## Introduction

**![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/34/)**

**About the game**

Demonschool is a new-style tactics RPG where motion equals action. Defeat big weirdos in between the human and demon worlds as Faye and her misfit companions, while navigating university life on a mysterious island.

Key Features

- **Tactics**: Rewind any action during the planning phase and create combos to dispatch your foes.

- **Story**: A light horror narrative told through huge avatars with side quests aplenty.

- **Schedule**: Plan your school schedule to level your characters and build their skills.

- **Friendship**: 15 characters with whom to build relationships.


The game was released on 11/19/2025 for PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series S/X, Nintendo Switch and PC.

## Week 11

This game uses a calendar system where the main events are split between three periods of the day: **Morning, Evening, Night**. Each time you complete a main objective, the period of the day will advance.

**Week 11**

**Monday**

**Morning**

After the cutscene ends you start at the boat. There are some NPCs you can talk to and an objective pin on the character ahead. Talk to her to meet Namako, another student who is heading toward the school on the island.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/35/)

> **Note:** Concluding main objectives advances the day.

Once you talk to Namako, a cutscene will trigger and it will advance to Friday.

* * *

**Friday**

**Morning**

Faye arrives at the island. After the cutscene ends the first battle will trigger.

**![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/36/)**

The battle starts with the planning phase, in which only you can take actions. Each action spends Action Points. The more actions one character makes, the more expensive each one becomes, so it is advised to rotate between characters.

**Target Turn:** 3 – In order to get the full score, the player must win the battle in the number of turns displayed in the top-left corner without any friendly characters dying.

**Seal Target:** 8 enemies – In order to win the battle, the player must defeat the number of enemies and position a character at the end of the board marked with the circles.

**Enemies:** Gangster.

The Gangster is the first enemy that you fight. He walks 2 tiles seeking opponents. Deals 1 damage and knocks the target back 1 tile.

**Attack:** Dark; **Resist:** Flood; **Weak:** Flame, Metal.

**Movement:** Your characters always move the full distance possible, marked by the yellow tiles. There is also a sidestep marked by the blue tile, which allows the player to move one tile to the side and then make another movement while spending only the Action Points of 1 movement. The red solid tile indicates that the character will attack the enemy positioned on that tile.

**Faye** is the only character available that can attack enemies, dealing 1 damage and pushing them back 1 tile. She is also equipped with her **Special**, dealing 2 Almighty damage (hitting the weakness of any enemy), and **refunds the Action Points if it kills an enemy**.

**Namako** passes through enemies, pulling them 1 tile while stunning and reducing their defense. Her Special creates an explosion of the Flood element, which covers 9 tiles in a square-shaped pattern.

You can rewind before finishing your turn by pressing the rewind button ( **L1 or LB**) and advance actions with ( **R1 or RB**).

**Combo Attacks** – Characters can deal more damage if they push or pull an enemy into other friendly characters. This will imbue the attack with the element of that character.

After you finish your turn, hold the action button. The characters will begin their actions, then the enemies will make theirs.

If you lose a character, once you win the battle they will return to the party.

**Defeat conditions:** You lose the battle if all of your characters are dead or if the barrier behind the allies is broken and an enemy passes through it.

After finishing the battle, a cutscene will trigger and you will advance to the next period of the day.

* * *

**Evening**

Faye will be at the Town Square. You can now move to other locations, the Harbor and the Entryway. There you will only find NPCs with short dialogue.

Back in the Town Square, there are some NPCs you can talk to. Talking with Namako will advance the story.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/37/)

* * *

**Night**

The new objective is to go to the School Gym Assembly.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/38/)

After the Dean welcomes the students and explains the school, Faye and Namako will go to the Town Alley.

There, a new battle will start.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/39/)

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 6 enemies

**Enemies:** Gangsters, Possessed Student

Here a new enemy is introduced the Possessed Student – Walks 2 tiles seeking opponents. Deals 1 damage.

**Attack:** Spirit; **Resist:** Flame; **Weak:** Earth ,Root.

This fight is pretty similar to the previous one. The new enemy presented is basically the same but without the knockback of the gangster.

After defeating them, the time will move to the next week.

## Week 10

#### **Monday**

**Morning**

A bunch of new locations will be available, some of them will have Side Quests available. You can check by the locations marked with " **?**" or " **!**" on the map menu.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/45/)

**Cemetery:**

- **Pet the Dog** – this is a side quest that can be done once almost every day.

**Town Square:**

- **Census Taker** – an NPC that informs you of the number of inhabitants on the island.
- **Drop a Coin in the Fountain** – another side quest that can be done once every day.

**Classroom:** Main Objective – talk with Professor Vincent. He will assign the objective of investigating a cursed tape that kills anybody who watches it within 3 days.

* * *

**Evening**

**Main Objective:**

- If you did the other side quests, you can go to the Computer Lab to advance the story. Faye will talk to Ti in the Gym, where she will prompt you to deadlift. A dialogue will appear where you have to choose between lifting the barbell and putting it back down 6 times. After that, they will move to the Entryway, where they will find a student with more information. Then they will go to the Harbor, where they will encounter gangsters.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/41/)

**Target Turn:** 2

**Seal Target:** 12 enemies

**Enemies:** Gangsters

This fight will have the Gangsters spawning aligned. Use Namako and side-step behind Faye, then stun them. After that, use Faye to make a combo attack. Next, use Namako to side-step and stun the other gangsters, and use Faye to defeat one more.

In the second turn, both characters should have defeated enough enemies to have their specials full. Use them to kill the rest of the enemies and proceed to the end of the board to finish the fight.

* * *

**Night**

Some new places will be available for you to explore.

**Main Objective:**

- Once you are ready go to the Town Alley and start the story mission. There you will meet **Destin**, who will join the upcoming fight.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/42/)

**Target Turn:** 2

**Seal Target:** 10 enemies

**Enemies:** Gangsters

**Destin** is a brawler like Faye. He is resistant to Curse-type attacks and weak against Spirit. His special knocks 1 enemy as far as possible, dealing 2 Flame DMG and hitting any enemies behind the target.

**1st turn:** You can clear the board in the first turn by using Namako to stun and align the enemies so Faye and Destin can finish them.

**2nd turn:** More enemies will spawn. Use Faye’s or Destin’s special to quickly defeat them.

After that, they will head toward the abandoned building, where they will be barred by the cops.

* * *

#### **Tuesday**

**Morning**

**Town Square:**

- **Drop a coin in the fountain.** You will be rewarded with **Untetherable** \- a technique that makes the character immune to Tether, a status immunity that won’t be necessary now.

> **Note:** Abilities need to be studied by 2 or 3 characters. They require credits and time, meaning the characters assigned to study an ability will enter a cooldown before being able to study another.

**Main Objective:**

- Head to the **Classroom** and talk to Professor Vincent. After the cutscene ends, Namako will officially enter the party, meaning you can use Faye and Namako to research the newly found ability.

* * *

**Evening**

**Main Objective:**

- Head to the **Classroom** again and talk to Namako to proceed. They will go to the Town Alley where they will meet a Shadowy Figure. There you will acquire the Hideous Doll.

* * *

**Night**

**Town Alley:**

- At night you will be able to access the **Store**, where a set of techniques and Aspect Shifts will be available to buy. These items require Opals as currency. Opals are gained naturally by winning battles and doing some side quests.

> **Note about the shop:** As the game progresses the prices will drop in the next few weeks, so don't buy anything now.

**Main Objective:**

- Once you are ready proceed to the Cemetery to progress the story. There they will meet Knute, who will be surrounded by Oossessed Students. Faye will throw the doll to distract them, starting a battle.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/43/)

**Target Turn:** 4

**Seal Target**: 15 enemies

**Enemies:** Possessed Students, Grunt

In this fight **Knute** will join as an ally. He is a Healer. They can’t normally attack except when part of a combo. He can heal allies, and if the ally has full health, the ally will be buffed, gaining Attack Up during that turn.

Knute is resistant to Spirit and weak to Dark. His special heals 3 tiles in a straight line for 1 HP, and fully heals himself.

A new enemy is introduced here: the Grunt. These enemies have 1 HP, only move forward 2 tiles, and deal 1 Curse DMG. Their main objective is to break barriers; if successful, they will try to move beyond the border of the board, triggering a defeat, so they can be ignored on the beginning of the fight.

**1st turn:** 3Possessed Students and 1 Grunt will spawn, since the Possessed Students are 1 tile apart from each other, use Namako to combo with Faye.

**2nd turn:** a bunch of enemies will spawn. Using Faye, try to dispatch multiple enemies by hitting a line of targets, and use Namako to stun them for the next turn.

**3rd turn:** you should be able to defeat the rest of the enemies, finishing the battle.

* * *

#### **Wednesday**

**Morning**

**Town Square:**

- **Drop a coin in the fountain**
- **Pet the Dog** – it should reward you with 1 Opal.

**Main Objective:**

- Head to the Classroom and talk to Destin to proceed the story.

* * *

**Evening**

**Main Objective:**

- Go to the **Cemetery** to encounter Destin and Knute surrounded by possessed students.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/46/)

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 12 enemies

**Enemies:** Possessed Student

**1st turn:** Use Knute to buff Destin’s attack to wipe out the line of enemies while comboing with Destin and Namako, then stun the rest and have Faye finish them off.

**2nd turn:** More enemies will spawn in 2 groups, one far from the other. You can repeat the same strategy or use Knute to buff both Destin and Faye. Using them to clear enemies and Namako to stun the rest.

**3rd turn:** Defeat the rest of enemies and seal the portal to finish the fight.

After that, Destin and Knute will leave. Pangxie will arrive, saying that he will watch the tape tonight.

* * *

**Night**

**Main Objective:**

- Go to the **Abandoned School Gate**. There, the guards will stop Faye and Namako. Destin and Knute will arrive, fleeing from the gangsters, and a battle will start.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/47/)

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 20 enemies

**Enemies:** Gangster, Grunt

**1st turn:** Use the diagonal to clear the arena of most enemies, including the gangsters, which are the strongest enemies in this battle because they have 2 HP.

**2nd turn:** A bunch of Grunts and a few gangsters will spawn. Try to focus on the gangsters, leaving some grunts for the next turn.

**3rd turn:** Defeat the remaining Grunts and you should be able to seal the fight.

After this battle, **Destin** and **Knute** will join Faye’s party.

* * *

#### **Thursday**

**Morning**

**Town Square:**

- **Drop a coin in the fountain** – there will be a small bag of Opals in the water. Pick it up to receive 10 Opals.

**Main Objective:**

- Go to the **Classroom** and talk with Namako for the Main Objective.

* * *

**Evening**

More side quests will be available in the following locations:

**Cemetery:**

- **I Got in a Fight with a Crow** – just talk to the NPC in the cemetery.

**Harbor:**

- **Knute Found Something Weird** – once you talk to him, you will be prompted with a choice between Namako and Destin. The character you choose will give RP (relationship points), leveling up Knute and the chosen character.

**Main Objective:**

- Once you complete the side quests, go to the **Entryway** for the Main Objective. After that, the group will head toward the location Destin mentioned. There they will find a crowd of possessed students who will fight them.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/48/)

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 20 enemies

**Enemies:** Grunt, Possessed Students

**1st turn:** You should be able to clear the board by using Namako and one of the brawlers to clear a line of enemies, then using Knute to buff the brawler to defeat the others.

**2nd turn:** More enemies will spawn. Luckily they are grouped, so you can defeat another bunch.

**3rd turn:** Finish off the required enemies to be able to seal. If you have some Action Points left, you can defeat extra grunts to get more Opals.

* * *

**Night**

**Main Objective:**

- Return to the Entryway to go after the Clubhouse key. After the cutscene ends, some gangsters will appear.

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 10 enemies

**Enemies:** Gangster, Possessed Student

In this fight, the enemies will be more scattered across the battlefield. Use Namako to align them so you can use Faye or Destin to take out multiple enemies at once.

* * *

#### **Friday**

**Morning**

**Town Square:**

- **Drop a coin on the fountain** – once you interact with the fountain, you will be asked if you had taken the opals. Answering yes will reward you with the **Thief** ability – a technique that makes that character get more loot from defeated enemies.

**Main Objective:**

- Go back to the Entryway and start the story mission.

* * *

**Evening**

**Main Objective:**

- Go to the Classroom and talk to Namako to proceed. From there, they will ask the Shadowy Figure for a key and return to Professor Vincent.

* * *

**Night**

**A new Side Quest will be available**

**Town Square:**

- **Bell Ringer** – After speaking with the Bell Ringer in the **Town Square**, a Grunt will appear, starting a battle.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/49/)

**Target Turn:** 3

**Seal Target:** 10 enemies

**Enemies:** Balloon, Grunt, Gangster (Possessed), Possessed Student, Regurgitator, Undead Dragoon

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/50/)

New enemies are introduced here, the most important being:

**Regurgitator** – a stationary enemy that can spit acid across the entire field in a straight line, causing poison to any character hit by it or who walks on the affected tile. This enemy is resistant to flame and weak to earth and gem. The Regurgitator drops an acid pool where it is defeated.

Poison is a status where the affected character takes 1 HP damage at the end of the turn.

**Balloon** – a demon that walks 1 tile seeking a target. Once it reaches one, it will arm itself as a bomb on the next round. On the following turn, it will explode, dealing 1 poison damage across a large area around it.

**Gangster** (Possessed) – 2 HP. The big difference from the normal variation is that he walks 2 tiles and has a perpendicular attack that covers 3 tiles. His attack is Dark. He is resistant to earth and weak to flood and metal.

**1st Turn:** A Balloon, 3 Grunts, 2 Possessed Gangsters and 2 Regurgitators will spawn, you can knock back the Balloon on the left side of the board making him arm himself to explode in the next turn then move out of the danger zone and focus on the Regurgitators

**2nd turn:** A new enemy will spawn: the Undead Dragoon – an enemy that can travel long distances on the map by jumping. At the beginning of the turn, he marks the trajectory where he will land, causing 1 Dark DMG and pushing all combatants 1 tile. This enemy has 3 HP.

Try to focus on the Regurgitators or the Undead Dragoon, as they are the immediate threats. Poison by itself isn’t threatening since you have 3 HP and the target turn is 3, but paired with other enemies it can lead to a character’s death, so act with some caution.

**3rd turn:** A Possessed Student (Bomb) with 2 HP will appear. Once defeated, they explode in a + pattern, meaning you can safely attack them from diagonals. Their element is Curse, resistant to earth and weak to flood and blood.

Defeat the rest of the enemies and proceed to seal the fight.

**Main Objective:**

- After completing the Side Quest, go to the **Abandoned Wing** for the main ojective. There you will start your first Boss Battle.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/51/)

**VHS Demon**

**Boss HP:** 16

**Target Turn:** 4

**Enemies:** Grunt, Possessed Student

Boss Battles act a little different as they only take 1 HP per hit, not caring if your character has buffed attacks. Also, they can be damaged by Healers.

As soon as the battle starts, a large area will be marked. This indicates where the boss will move.

3 tiles will be marked on the map — these indicate where Possessed Students will fall.

**1st turn**

Is basically free attacks. Position the team members so they can attack the boss while avoiding the marked areas. After attacking, try to position them close to the next large area where the boss will move so they don’t need to spend AP (action points) moving next turn.

**2nd turn**

Some Grunts and a Possessed Student will spawn. The boss will throw some TVs on the map. Since the Grunts will probably be far from the barrier, you can focus on the boss and defeat the Possessed Student to make things safer for your characters.

**3rd turn**

By this turn you should have, or be close to having, your special attack available. Use Faye’s special to clear some peons while repositioning her close to the boss. Then use the other characters to hit the boss.

**4th turn**

Now you can just attack the boss. If there is AP available after depleting the boss’s life, use it to defeat some enemies and gain more opals.

After defeating the boss, they will find and destroy the haunted tape. The game will advance toward the weekend.

* * *

#### **Weekend**

In the weekend the day mechanic is a little different: there are no 3 periods of the day, so make sure to complete any Side Quests before completing the Main Objective.

New Side Quests will be available in:

**Lighthouse:**

- **Mysterious Shrine: Pay tribute** – This is a side quest similar to petting the dog or dropping a coin in the fountain. It will be available almost every day.

**Town Square :**

- **Drop a Coin in the Fountain.**

**Rooftop :**

- **Namako’s Tamagos** \- Speak with Namako to complete this side quest and gain relationship points.

**Main Objective:**

Once you are ready, head to the **Clubhouse Exterior** to proceed with the story.

Now talk with **Knute** to meet the van man. From now on, each weekend the player can rent a tape from his collection. There are 5 choices, and they don’t have any impact on gameplay — they only show a review of the chosen movie in the newspaper at the beginning of the next week:

After renting the movie, the next objective will be the **quiz** in the **Classroom.** This is the last objective of the weekend.

Every weekend you will take a Quiz with themes related to the game, with 3 possible answers: weird, ok, and correct. Picking the correct answer will reward you with Research Credits used to study techniques. The other answers reward opals.

![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/52/)

**1st Quiz**

**1st question:** What’s my name?

Correct answer – Thomas Vincent

**2nd question:** Do the island and school have the same name?

Correct answer – Yes they do.

**3rd question:** So what in fact is the island’s name?

Correct answer – Hemsk

**4th question:** How many members are needed to form a club?

Correct answer – Four

**5th question:** Who is the school’s dean?

Correct answer – Asatiani

**6th question:** If you fail in school where do you go?

Correct answer – Prison

After answering all the questions, the story will progress to the next week.

## Week 9

#### **Monday**

**Morning**

**Cemetery:**

- **Pet the Dog**

**Town Square:**

- **Drop a coin in the fountain**
- **Census Taker:** Know the number of island inhabitants.
- **Newspaper** – Now each week there will be a newspaper containing:
- • An article and the photo of the previous boss you have fought
- • A movie review from Knute
- • A short article

**Lighthouse:**

- **Mysterious Shrine – Pay tribute**

**New items will be available at the store:**

- **Furniture Sets:** These are cosmetics used to customize the Clubhouse, changing the overall look of the room.

The first set available will be: Electronics – 2500
- **Furniture Items:** These are cosmetics used to customize individual pieces of furniture. These items are exclusive to their sets, indicated by the prefix name.


**Relationship missions** should become available now. Once a friendly character fills their relationship bar, a mission becomes available to level up their relationship with Faye, rewarding new techniques. These missions are marked with a heart icon.

> **Note:** Missions become available depending on the character’s relationship level. Some missions may only appear later in the game.

- **Namako** – Talking with Namako in the **Clubhouse Exterior** will lead Faye to the Karaoke, where a new minigame becomes available.
- The **Karaoke** is a choice-based minigame where you must find the hidden topic among 3 phrases (e.g., the word **cat** can appear as “catatonic”).
- The first time you play, choosing a wrong answer repeats the question with new options.

**Boss HP:** 25

**Target Turn:** 7

**Enemies:** Jackboot.

This fight takes place on a **circular board**, which changes the movement dynamic significantly. **Jackboots will spawn**, and ignoring them isn’t an option since they can kick your units off the arena. Brawler-type characters are very effective here because they can remove Jackboots quickly.

**Aina excels in this battle** thanks to her Special, which allows her to travel across the board and strike multiple tongues in one action.

**Boss Attacks:**

- **Tongue Strike:** Appears at random. Deals **1 damage** and **knocks back 1 tile**.

- **Tongue Sweep:** An area attack.

- **Head Slam:** When you have struck _all_ active tongues, the boss will bash its head next turn, **breaking the floor 1 tile around the seal** and creating danger zones.


As the fight progresses, **more tongues will spawn each round** after being struck, so **positioning your characters around the board is crucial**.

Healers are optional — it’s possible to win without one — but some luck is required since tongue spawns are random.

**Recommended Abilities**

- **Fleet Feet:** +1 movement tile.

- **Knockback Up:** Helps deal with Jackboots.

- **Crab Walk:** Extra sidestep tile.

- **Filler Killer:** Great for phasers to hit enemies and tongues positioned 1 tile apart.

- **Head Start + Stoic:** Faster Special gauge fill and +1 damage when gauge is full.


**Turn 1:** A Jackboot spawns and a tongue will strike, marking a **yellow zone** (knockback 1 tile).

The **red zone** shows tiles the boss will break next turn.

Use a brawler to attack the **Jackboot** while also hitting the **tongue** if possible. Try to defeat the Jackboot immediately.

**Turn 2:** 2 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn.

Attack the tongues and either:

- defeat the Jackboot, **or**

- move far enough away so it can’t knock your characters out.


**Turn 3:** 3 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn. Use **Aina’s Special** here if available — she can strike **half the board**, hitting every tongue.

Defeat the Jackboot and avoid the marked danger zones.

From this point onward, begin **spreading your team across the arena**.

**Turn 4:** 4 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn.

Attack the tongues and defeat or escape the Jackboot.

**Turn 5:** 5 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn.

Use Specials to hit **multiple tongues** and try to **push the Jackboot off the map**.

**Turn 6:** 6 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn.

Repeat the previous strategy. With Aina or Destin, you should be able to strike multiple tongues in a single action.

**Turn 7:** 7 tongues and 1 Jackboot spawn.

Use Aina to quickly defeat multiple tongues, push the Jackboot off the arena, and finish the fight by striking **all tongues** to end the battle.

**Final Boss: Grandfather**

- This battle consists of a series of three fights taking place one after the other.

- In the first two fights, you can manage your loadouts.

- In the last fight, loadouts cannot be changed, so make sure to equip your characters appropriately before entering.


**Demon’s Hoof**

**![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/281/)**

**Boss HP:** 10

**Turn Target:** 4

**Enemies:** Demon Hoof, Possessor, Handslinger, Dark Sorcerer, Regurgitator, infernal soul

**Strategy:** Bring **one or two Phase characters** and a **Modifier**.

- **Aina** is excellent, as her super allows her to move across the entire board.

- **Kestrel** is recommended as well, being one of the Phase characters that can damage enemies while passing through them.

- The **Modifier** can be anyone, but **Knute** or **Pangxie** are very strong choices because when they heal a fully healthy ally, they grant **Attack Up**.


**Recommended Skills:**

- **Kestrel:** Permabuff (+1 ATK) and Crossslash to clear multiple enemies.

- **Aina:** Filler Killer and Stun for better crowd control.

- **Modifier:** Big Plus to buff two allies on the first turn; Paladin I or II to resist attacks; Looper for extra mobility (optional); and potentially resistances like Resist Fire or specific enemy type resistances.


**1st Turn:** 3 or 4 enemies from the list above may spawn. This first fight is relatively easy. As soon as the battle starts, an area will be marked— **avoid it**, as it causes instant death. Reposition your characters to the sides of the marked area; if they are in front of or behind the boss’s foot, the stomp will push them to the edge of the arena.

You can also clear some enemies during this turn, but try to keep the team grouped so all members can attack the Hoof on the next turn.

**2nd Turn:** Focus on attacking the Hoof. Only engage peons if absolutely necessary.

**3rd Turn:** A new area will be marked, and more enemies will spawn. Prioritize enemies that can **stun or possess** your characters. You can ignore burn and poison, as this fight is relatively quick. Once the required enemies are defeated, reposition your team near the sides of the marked area.

**4th Turn:** Attack with all team members to defeat the boss.

**Second Fight**

**Demon Mass**

**![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/282/)**

**Boss HP:** 25

**Turn Target:** 5

**Enemies:** Hand Slinger, Jackboot

**Strategy:** As with other boss fights, it is recommended to bring **at least one Modifier** so your team can last through multiple turns. Similar to the previous fight, **Kestrel** and **Aina** are strong choices. Ideally, have **2 Modifiers**, one for each team; the rest of your team composition is flexible.

**Recommended Skills:**

- **No Debuff, Fire Heal, Resist Fire, Demon Crown** – solid defensive options for your peon killers.

- **Note:** with **Fire Heal** and **Demon Crown**, avoid curing status effects too quickly. Let them take damage from other units to prevent burn from spreading to the rest of your team.

- **Counter Attack** – pairs well with **Permabuff**. If your Modifier applies the **Paladin shield**, you’ll have a strong tank capable of handling peons.

- **Stunner and Crossslash** – for **Kestrel** to crowd-control enemies.

- **Filler Killer and No Debuff** – for **Aina**, so she can safely move through burning tiles.

- **Paladin I & II, Dispel, Big Plus** – ideal for Modifiers. **Pangxie** and **Knute** are excellent choices, as they can protect and buff allies while **Dispel** allows them to safely move through burning tiles to attack the boss.

- **Pangxie with Paladin II and Big Plus** – particularly useful in the third fight.

- **Poison** – for **Namako**, allowing her to deal damage while stunning enemies.


**Boss Behavior:** The boss will move to another map each turn, leaving a **trail of lava** that deals **2 damage** and inflicts **Weak** on anything in its path.

Since you have more action points, focus on the team nearest to the boss while keeping the rest of your team safe and positioned for attacks.

**1st Turn: Handslingers and Jackboots will spawn in both boards.**

- **Bottom Board:** Use **Namako** to stun priority peons, such as the **Handslingers**. Use your **Modifier** to buff one of the allies. Assign another character to deal with the **Jackboots**.
- Use the remaining character(s) to score hits on the boss.

- **Top Board:** If enemies are far away, you can ignore them initially. Keep in mind **Handslingers** have a **5-tile attack range**; reposition your allies to avoid the boss’s attacks while staying close enough to hit it. Use the **Modifier** to provide shields to allies as needed.

**2nd Turn: Some enemies may spawn,**

- **Bottom Board:** Focus on attacking enemies on the **Top Board** if possible. Any remaining AP can be used to clear weaker enemies on the bottom board.
- **Top Board:** Prioritize **dealing damage to the boss** rather than fighting peons, especially if your allies are shielded. Only engage peons that can **grab or kill your allies**.

> Repeat this cycle and you should defeat the boss by the **5th turn**.

**3rd Turn:** More **Handslingers** and **Jackboots** will spawn in both boards.

- **Bottom Board:** Use **special attacks** to take out stronger peons. Try to land as many hits as possible on the boss.
- **Top Board:** Heal any allies with low HP or inflicted with **Burn**. Clear some enemies while positioning your team near the **red marked zone** for the next turn.

**4th Turn:** Additional **Handslingers** and **Jackboots** will spawn in both boards.

- **Bottom Board:** Clear enemies and reposition your team for the next turn.
- **Top Board:** Apply **shields** if necessary. Focus attacks on the boss.

**5th Turn:** More enemies will spawn. Focus entirely on **attacking the boss** to finish the fight.

* * *

**Third Fight**

**Demon Head**

**![](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ffaq/80/283/)**

**Boss HP:** 12

**Turn Target:** 12

**Enemies:** Zombie, Grunt

This fight is a **3-team battle** with **5 members per team**, and you **cannot choose the loadout**. This is why it’s recommended to carefully **choose the skills at the beginning of the first fight**.

**Available Characters**

- **1st Team:** Faye, Knute, Destin, Aina, Mercy

- **2nd Team:** Namako, Ti, Pangxie, Henk, Varka

- **3rd Team:** Kestrel, Jem, Ocean Fossa, Primrose


In this battle, **skills are fixed** and cannot be changed; they are the ones **previously selected**.

**Battle Notes:**

- Each team will fight for **3 turns**.

- The **1st team fights twice**, while the other teams fight once per cycle.

- Proper skill selection before this fight is crucial to achieving the **target turn**


**1st Team**

**1st Turn:** On the first turn, a bunch of **Grunts** and some **Zombies** will spawn. Two lines will be marked on the ground where the **boss** will shoot **lasers**, covering the entire area and leaving the tiles **burning**. Three tiles will be marked with a different symbol, indicating that **crystals** will appear on the next turn.

You can fight some **peons** during this round, but try to position **at least two characters near the red marks** so they can strike the **crystals** in the next turn. Be careful with the **Zombies**, as they can inflict **Doom**.

**2nd Turn:** The **boss** will now mark a **cross-shaped pattern**, the **crystals** will spawn, and some enemies will reposition. Strike the **crystals**, and the cross-shaped mark will disappear while a new one appears. Try to move **at least two characters near the new mark**.

At the end of this turn, once all the **crystals** are destroyed, the **boss** will fall on the arena, letting its **tongue** strike the ground and **kill anything in its path**.

**3rd Turn:** Focus on attacking the **boss’s tongue**. You need to strike it **three times** to advance the battle to the next team

* * *

**2nd Team**

**1st Turn:** The **boss** will start with a **cross-shaped pattern** of attack. A bunch of **Zombies** will spawn along with a few **Grunts**.

This part can be troublesome, as the only characters that can damage multiple enemies in one turn are **Ti** (because of her knockback) and **Namako** if equipped with **Add Poison**. Moving across the map may put your team in reach of the **Zombies’ attacks**.

If **Pangxie** has **Big Plus** and **Paladin Shield** equipped, use them to protect **Namako** and **Ti**. **Henk** can be used to place **barriers** to slow the **Zombies’ progress**.

The first turn should be used to **clear as many Zombies as possible**.

**2nd Turn:** The **boss** will mark **two straight lines** for its next attack. Once you destroy the **crystals**, the attack will be canceled.

Focus on **destroying the crystals** while clearing the map of some **Zombies**. If the **Grunts** aren’t close to destroying a **barrier**, you can safely ignore them.

**3rd Turn:** Now **attack the boss’s tongue three times** to advance the battle to the next team.

* * *

**3rd Team**

**1st Turn:** The **boss** will start with the **straight-line pattern**. A bunch of **Phase Grunts** will spawn along with a few **Zombies**.

Use **Kestrel** with the **Crossslash** ability to defeat multiple enemies at once. You can also use **Primrose’s special** to copy the attack of a **Phase Grunt**, allowing her to pass through multiple enemies.

**2nd Turn:** Destroy the **crystals** and try to defeat the remaining **Zombies**.

**3rd Turn:** Attack the **boss’s tongue three times** to advance to the next phase.

* * *

**1st Team**

The **1st team** will return to the positions you placed them previously.

**1st Turn:** The **boss** will mark a **cross-shaped attack**. This turn will spawn **Grunts, Phase Grunts, and Zombies**. Be careful, as an ally can get surrounded if they are too far from the group.

By now, all characters should have **special attacks** available: **Mercy** and **Aina’s specials** cover the entire map. **Destin** has infinite knockback if the enemy is near. **Faye**, if she defeats multiple enemies with her special and has **Quick Charge**, can almost recharge it immediately.

Focus on defeating the **Zombies** and avoiding the **Phase Grunts’ attacks**.

**2nd Turn:** Attack the **crystals** and defeat some remaining **enemies**.

**3rd Turn:** Strike the **boss’s tongue three times** to defeat the boss.
