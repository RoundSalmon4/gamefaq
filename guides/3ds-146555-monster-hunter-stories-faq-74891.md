## Introduction

This Monster Hunter Stories guide lists pictures of all the eggs found in the game, serving as a visual aid for those looking for a specific egg/species. There are three page lists altogether.

In the first list, monsties are listed in the numerical order that they appear in the game. This is to make it easier for anyone to find the monster they need regardless of language. It will be useful for someone who's at the end of the game and is missing one or two eggs. There are 71 monsties altogether on this page, though not all monsties are available on every version.

I've also put lists in order of location and in order of genus, which are more helpful as you're playing through the game.

#### Additional Notes

- Glavenus is only available in the English versions on 3DS/mobile by collecting egg fragments from online play (using the MH Gen ruleset). However, in the 2024 remastered version, you can get Glavenus eggs before post-game by completing the "Generation Gap" rank 5 Special Subquest from the request board. The eggs you collect from the den after defeating the den boss are guaranteed to be Glavenus eggs. Otherwise, you can find Glavenus eggs in the wild post-game as shown above.
- Kurenai Goukami is only available on 3DS/mobile.
- Epona is only available on 3DS.
- Rajang, Teostra and Kushala Daora are not available as hatchable monsties in the English versions on 3DS/mobile. However, they are in the English release of the 2024 remaster.
- Kumashira was a JP only monstie that could only be obtained by putting fragments together from a JP exclusive tournament. It didn't have its own separate egg, but I put the image of what it looks like when you put all its pieces together. It is not available in the remaster.
- Glavenus, Kurenai Goukami, Epona and Kumashira do not appear as enemies in the game, so they do not have entries in the Monstiepedia. Thus, they have no Monstiepedia number.
- Kiranico has additional egg patterns for Silver Rathalos and Gold Rathian, but the game only ever generates the first colour on the list, so I haven't put the extra egg colours here.
- You'll receive a Gold Rathian egg or a Silver Rathalos egg as a reward for completing 19F or 29F of the Tower of Illusions respectively. However, these eggs have genes that are always fixed in the same position. If you want Silver Rathalos or Gold Rathian eggs with genes in different positions, you must get them from the subquests instead.
- The total number of monsties on this page is 71, but since the collaboration monsties are not in the remastered version, there are in fact 68 monsties in the remastered version.
