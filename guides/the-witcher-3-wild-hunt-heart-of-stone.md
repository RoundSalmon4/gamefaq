## Gameplay Basics

### Combat Techniques

#### Melee Combat

Melee attacks are where Geralt will be spending most of his time in combat and is your largest single source of damage. Subsequently players will be focusing more on melee than any other form of combat.

Geralt comes equipped with a pair of swords with which he can dish out DPS – a Silver Sword for fighting monsters and a Steel Sword for fighting humanoid opponents. Players can switch between swords at any point, but Geralt usually pulls out the relevant sword as he enters combat, so in most circumstances won't need to worry about it.

Geralt starts off with a standard light and heavy attack but can unlock additional, more powerful melee abilities if you spend skill points in the combat tree. The attacks include:

Fast/Heavy Attacks – Geralt can perform a light attack or a heavy attack. Light attacks are slightly faster, but do reduced damage. Heavy attacks on the other hand take longer but will dish out significantly more damage. As Geralt's wind-up animation for the fast/heavy attacks leaves him open to counterattacks in the interim it is recommended to only use heavy attacks on stunned enemies or those unaware of your presence.

These can be further enhanced for bonus critical strike %, bleeding effects and damage by sticking points into the respective **Fast Attack** and **Heavy Attack** tabs in the **Combat skill Tree**.

Additional melee-based abilities are unlocked once you start investing some points in the melee portion of the Combat Skill Tree. These abilities include:

- **Whirl –** This is a spinning attack that will do area of effect damage to any enemy within the immediate vicinity.
- **Rend –** This is a nasty overhead slash that will ignore armour and shields and do an enormous amount of damage.

#### Ranged Combat

Near the start of the game, Geralt is also gifted with a crossbow which can be used to perform ranged attacks against enemies. Whilst pulling out the crossbow to aim, time will slow a little giving you a slightly larger window in which to aim and fire.

Whilst crossbows remain your source of ranged damage during standard combat, their attack effects can be altered somewhat by the ammo that you choose to use with them. Different bolts can be crafted by armourers and will have different effects and damage bonuses.

Crossbow attacks can be further enhanced to slow time further whist aiming, increase critical hit damage/% and disable enemy special abilities for a brief time by investing points into the **Marksmanship** portion of the **Combat Skill Tree**.

#### Finisher Attacks

Once you have dealt enough damage to an enemy, a button prompt will appear above their heads. Hitting the button signified when in close proximity will trigger a stylish execution attack that will kill the enemy during a canned animation. On the lower difficulties Geralt is invincible during these animations, so if you are low on health it is possible to exploit a finisher to avoid taking damage.

> **Note:** Automatic finishers are enabled by default when you start a new game and as such you will not see the button prompts appear. They can be toggled on or off in the menu. This should be deactivated in the higher difficulty modes to avoid frustration!

#### Avoiding Attacks

Geralt has several ways in which he can avoid incoming enemy attacks or to limit or mitigate damage taken. Each of these techniques has its own benefits and drawbacks and experimenting with each in different situations will help figure out the right tactic for your gameplay style. Additional bonuses and abilities can be unlocked by investing points in the **Defence** portion of the **Combat Skill Tree**.

The default abilities include:

- **Blocking** – by blocking, in most circumstances Geralt can pretty much nullify the majority of standard melee attacks coming his way. Unfortunately, although it can reduce damage, it is not quite as effective when attempting to block incoming poison, fire or magical damage.
- **Staggering** – Geralt receives the ability to stagger most melee enemies near the start of the game. By timing your press of the block button to immediately before an enemy attack, Geralt can deflect and quickly counter with a hit that will also stun the recipient temporarily. This will leave them open for an additional follow up attack. Note that deflect/staggering will only work against melee attacks.
- **Dodging** – Geralt has the ability to do perform a quick side/back step to move out of danger quickly, but still maintain a short distance from a foe. This is incredibly useful for dodging incoming, unblockable enemy attacks during melee combat or during boss fights where blocking is not always an option. Pulling off a successful dodge will also give you a perfect opportunity to immediately follow up with a counterattack.
- **Rolling** – Rolling is similar to dodging in that it can get you out of a pinch fairly quickly. Unfortunately, it is somewhat less useful than the dodge as it will put more distance between Geralt and the enemy. This is much less useful against human enemies, but can be a lifesaver against larger boss monsters with unstoppable charging or area of effect knock back attacks.

Additional defensive abilities are unlocked once you start investing some points in the Defensive portion of the Combat Skill Tree. These abilities include:

- **Deflect Arrows** \- By timing your press of the block button to immediately before an enemy arrow/bolt hits, Geralt can deflect it to avoid taking damage.

### Magical Signs

In addition to utilising ranged and melee combat abilities, Geralt is also privy to using magical signs. These signs have a range of functions with some causing nasty area of effect attacks, others buffing Geralt and others being useful both in and out of combat. These sign attacks will consume Geralt's **Stamina Bar** which is the circular bar located below the health bar in the top left of the screen.

After a brief introduction, all five of Geralt's Sign abilities are available from the start of the game, however additional bonus effects and alternate modes can be purchased from the **Sign Skill Tree**. All abilities within the tree can be upgraded multiple times to increase their effectiveness.

These Signs Abilities include:

- **AARD** – The AARD sign has many uses. Its base form acts as a kind of Jedi-style force push which can knock down enemies or cause them to stagger temporarily. Outside of combat, it can be used to knock down weak walls.
- **AXII**– In its base form, AXII allows Geralt to temporarily stun individual human/monster/beast enemies. This can also be used out of combat during conversations, usually to get your way or to calm animals (you can tame and ride horses doing this).
- **IGNI**– As a starting ability, IGNI is your major damage sign. When used it sends out a wave of fire immediately in front of Geralt. This will damage enemies and has a chance to light them on fire briefly stunning them and causing damage over time. This can be used out of combat to light torches and to complete several quests.
- **QUEN**– This sign is a mostly defensive one. In its base form, the sign creates a protective magical barrier around Geralt that will drastically improving his defence against incoming attacks. Once activated, the ability will slowly drain the Stamina Bar until it is completely depleted.
- **YRDEN**– The YRDEN sign creates a magical trap on the ground. When stepped in by an enemy, certain effects can occur, in its base form, the YRDEN trap will slightly slow enemy movement and pull ghostly creatures into a physical form.

### Alchemy, Potions, Oils and Bombs

As a Witcher, Geralt is well versed in how to find reagents and concoct a range of alchemical-themed items which can provide him with a range of buffs and grant him advantage in battle. Recipes can be purchased from vendors, given as the occasional reward during a quest or found in chests or other lootable objects.

These recipes, once acquired can be crafted from the Alchemy tab of the menu. Fortunately, after they have been crafter for the first time, Geralt will remember how to make them (he's good like that) and as long as you have sufficient alcohol in your possession, will automatically create a fresh batch every time you meditate.

The different types of alchemy formulae include:

- **Bombs** – There are a range of bombs that Geralt can craft with numerous effects. They can be equipped and are utilised like grenades.
- **Potions** – A wide range of different potions can be used to assist Geralt both in and out of combat. Potions offer up a wide ranging assortment of effects that can to mention but a few: allow breathing in poisoned areas, to regenerating health and increasing damage output. sing potions will increase **Geralt's Toxicity Bar** (filling this too much will result in a loss of health).
- **Oils** – Oils are used to enhance Geralt's weapons temporarily. For the most part these are used to increase attack damage or efficiency during combat and are often related to fighting against specific enemy types.
- **Decoctions** – similar to oils, decoctions are special potions that give bonus attributes, perks or qualities when fighting against specific enemy types. Typically decoctions are found in the environment and in my experience you'll locate them near where you fight new enemy types for the first time.
- **Alchemy Reagents** – For some of the more advanced recipes, players are able to craft some reagents themselves. These include Mutagens (which can be used to enhance Geralt's abilities) and other more hard to find constructed reagents.

### Advanced Combat Tips

Whilst the standard attacks that Geralt uses to dish out damage are all well and good, there are a number of abilities or items that can when used in conjunction can give you a little extra edge in combat.

#### Environmental Attacks

In addition to the standard arsenal of attacks that Geralt has at his disposal, he can also utilise various elements of the environment in conjunction with his sign magic to help during combat. These include:

- **High points and AARD** – Geralt can use his AARD force push attack to launch enemies to their deaths from cliffs, buildings or from other high locations.
- **Beehives and AARD** – Whilst wandering in the wilderness you will come across beehives on trees. These can be shot with the crossbow for Honeycomb, destroyed with IGNI and can be exploited to damage enemies by utilising AARD. Using AARD will destroy the nest and the bees will then attack anything that comes close.
- **Swamp Gas and IGNI** – Whilst venturing in swamps, or sewers, or ancient ruins or a combination of the aforementioned locales, you'll occasionally come across swamp gas. Standing in this will damage Geralt, so it's a good idea to avoid getting too close to it. However, it also happens to be highly flammable. Using IGNI to ignite the gas will cause an explosion damaging or killing anything within a close proximity.
- **Barrels and IGNI** – When you come across bandit camps, guarded estates or abandoned settlements with hostile enemies, you'll often see barrels scattered around the area. Most barrels standing by themselves contain material that causes them to explode within a short time of being exposed to IGNI. As with swamp gas, these can be exploited to damage or kill enemies.

### Managing Bars: Health, Stamina, Adrenaline and Toxicity

Geralt has a number of bars that you will need to manage to keep on top of a range of issues affecting Geralt's performance both in combat and out. There are four gauge indicators that appear in the top left corner of the screen and each is associated with a different aspect of gameplay. They include:

#### Health Bar

The large red bar in the top left of the screen represents Geralt's current health. If this bar is entirely depleted, Geralt will fall and you'll need to reload your previous checkpoint. Health can be restored by eating **Food** or by consuming **Swallow Potions**. On the easier difficulties, meditating will fully restore your health but this will not work on

The amount of hit points in the health bar can be further increased by investing skill points into abilities related to boosting Geralt's total health pool ( **Survival Instinct**). It is also possible to allow regeneration of health over time ( **Sun and Stars**) which can be found in the **General Skill Tree**.

Depending on the armour equipped, the amount of damage received, and thus the health loss associated can be reduced.

#### Stamina

The Stamina bar refers to the yellow semi-circular bar located on the far left of the screen directly below the health bar. This bar is associated with both Geralt's ability to sprint and the use of Geralt's sign abilities.

Signs can only be used when the Stamina bar is full. The Stamina meter is depleted upon using a sign and afterwards will slowly fill over time to allow you to use another – it is, in essence a cooldown for your sign abilities to prevent spamming.

There are special abilities such as **Whirl** and **Rend** in the **Heavy/Quick Attack** portions of the **Combat Skill Tree** that will consume Stamina to perform a special attack.

#### Adrenaline

The adrenaline meter is located below the Health Bar and just to the left of the Stamina Bar. It is comprised of three small semicircles that will gradually fill whilst dishing out damage during combat and reduce when Geralt sustains damage. At the end of combat, the bar will drain to empty.

In its most basic form, the Adrenaline meter will give Geralt a slight boost to his damage – the fuller the bar is, the more bonus damage Geralt will be able to dish out. Along with the obvious health-related benefits, the bonus damage provided by Adrenaline encourages players to learn to properly use the roll and dodge moves Geralt has at his disposal.

There are several interesting Adrenaline-related skills on offer in the General Skill Tree. They include:

- **Focus** will further scale damage that you do to the amount of Adrenaline available, essentially improving the base form.
- **Adrenaline Burst** will increase the rate of Adrenaline generation by 5%. You'll also generate Adrenaline when using Signs.
- **Rage Management** enables you to utilise Adrenaline to cast Sign attacks if the Stamina bar is not sufficiently full enough.

Investing points in the **Combat Skill Tree** will have a passive effect of increasing % of Adrenaline generation.

#### Toxicity

The Toxicity meter can be located directly beneath the Health Bar. There is a Skull icon at its left hand side. It will fill as you use potions and decoctions and slowly drain over time. When your Toxicity level reaches 95% you will reach an unsafe level – Geralt will immediately lose a chunk of health and continue to lose health over time until the Toxicity level returns below 95%.

There are several interesting Toxicity-related skills on offer in the Alchemy Skill Tree. They include:

- **Heightened Tolerance** will allow you to add 1% per point invested towards your safe Toxicity threshold (95%) until it reaches 100%.
- **Acquired Tolerance** will provide a bonus increase to your Toxicity level for every alchemy potion that you learn.
- **Fast Metabolism** will greatly speed up the drain-out of the Toxicity meter.
- **Delayed Recovery** will increase your potion duration as long as you can maintain Toxicity levels above 90% of the safe threshold.
- **Metabolism Control** will increase your maximum Toxicity by 30.

Investing points in the **Alchemy Skill Tree** will have a passive effect of increasing % Toxicity Tolerance.

### Witcher Sense

Witcher Sense is a vision mode that we will be using extensively throughout the game. Similar to the special ‘Detective modes' in the Batman: Arkham games or the most recent Tomb Raider, Witcher Sense will highlight objects and areas of interest in the environment. In general the following are observable with Witcher Sense active:

- **Orange -** Chests, plants and other lootable containers/objects will be highlighted in orange.
- **Red -** Quest-related lootable objects or points of interest to be investigated are highlighted in red.
- **Ripples –** Enemies can be identified by ripples in the environment, allowing you to monitor their locations.

In addition to the visual advantage, Witcher Sense will also enhance Geralt's hearing, allowing him to eavesdrop on conversations. This comes in handy occasionally when trying to garner information during a quest or two.

### Exploration

The game's world is broken down into five maps – one for Kaer Morhan, Vizima, Velen, White Orchard and Skellige. On the maps, areas of interest are marked with a ‘?' until Geralt investigates to determine what it is. These then change to icons that include: Side-missions, fast travel points, dungeon entrances,vendors and side-tasks.

#### Travelling Efficiently

There are a few cool things you can do with the world maps to increase your efficiency at travelling around in-game:

##### Waypoints

During quests you'll receive objective markers that act as waypoints showing you exactly where you need to go to continue your quest. On the world map you can also set your own custom waypoint which will appear on the mini-map when you return to regular gameplay. You can then follow said waypoint to any destination of your choice.

Whilst waypoints are active, a white dotted trail will appear on the world map, giving you the quickest route to your destination. A small footprint icon will also appear by the mini-map with a number indicating your distance from the waypoint and arrows beside it telling you if it above or below your current position.

##### Fast Travel

As you explore the world you'll find fast travel signposts. As you ride or walk past them, they will be added to your wold map. By approaching one of these and interacting with it, you are able instantly select and travel to any previously discovered destination on the map where you have unlocked a fast travel point.

##### Horse Riding

You can summon Geralt's faithful horse, Roach at any time on the world map. Riding him around is much faster than Geralt's standard walking pace! If you get bored with him, you can use Geralt's **AXII** sign on wild horses to temporarily tame and ride them around!

The horse has three speeds:

- **Walking** – The slowest speed, but the most manoeuvrable.
- **Canter** – Hold down the run button and the horse will canter at a faster pace.
- **Gallop** – tap and then hold down the run button to gallop. This is the fastest setting.

A useful, yet unexplained feature of the horses is autopilot. If you hold the run button down to canter whilst on a road and do not touch the directional sticks, your horse will automatically run along the road you are on as if it were stuck there with glue – it may not always take the route you want at crossroads or forks though!

Whilst riding a horse, a Stamina meter will appear at the bottom of the screen. By tapping and then holding the run button, the horse will break into a gallop which will slowly consume the stamina bar. Once the bar is empty, the horse will revert to a walk until some of the Stamina is recharged.

> **Note:** Finding and equipping different saddles can increase Roach's total Stamina.

In addition to stamina, the horses also have a spook meter. If you get too close to enemies whilst on horseback and cannot escape the area quickly enough, the horse will get spooked, rear back and Geralt will fall to the ground. So avoid enemies!

##### Boating

You'll find numerous sailboats scattered about the waterways of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt. These can be used to quickly traverse bodies of water in a much quicker fashion than Geralt's normal swimming pace. To sail a boat, Geralt needs to hop on board and interact with the rear of the vessel.

In general boats have three speeds:

- **Slow** – The slowest speed, but the most manoeuvrable.
- **Fast** – Hold down the run button and the boat will accelerate.

Once in a boat, you'll be able to make out a silhouette of the boat located on the left side of the screen beneath Geralt's health bar (and other gauges, meters and bars of course). This starts off as white, indicating that there is no damage. Bumping into rocks or having enemies hit you can damage the boat. Damaged portions of the boat will be shown in red. If the boat takes too much damage it will sink and you'll be left with no option but to swim to shore.

### Gathering Experience and Levelling Up

As per your standard RPG experience players will earn experience points (XP) which can be accumulated to earn levels. Upon levelling, Geralt will be given a small boost to his base attributes and unlock a skill point. Skill points can then be invested in one of dour skill trees to unlock enhanced abilities, alternate attack types and some general stat bonuses.

In the Witcher 3 you'll gain XP via the following:

- Killing enemies,
- Progressing through story events
- Completing secondary-quests and Witcher Contracts.
- Completing side-tasks such as: Abandoned Settlements.
- Using AXII during conversations.
- Bribe people during conversations.

When you gain a level, you can use the opportunity to enhance Geralt's abilities. Within the character menu, you have the ability to peruse four skill trees and are able to purchase abilities (or ranks of abilities) by investing Skill Points. As is to be expected, the XP required to reach subsequent levels will increase as Geralt moves through the levels. The breakdown of XP required for certain levels is as such:

- Level 1 to Level 10 – 1,000XP per level.
- Level 11 – Level 20 – 1,500XP per level.
- Level 21 and onwards – 2,000XP per level.

At this point, the level cap for Geralt is level 70. This is only obtainable after completing the game and importing your save into New Game Plus mode.

#### Skill Points and Skill Trees

The majority of Skill Points will be earned as a reward for when Geralt earns sufficient XP to level up, however you'll also be able to obtain a single Skill Point each time you discover and using a Place of Power for the first time – so stay on the lookout for those as you explore the world!

As most RPG gamers might have guessed, these Skill Points can then be invested to unlock new abilities for Geralt in one of four Skill Trees. There are three main Skill Trees available to Geralt in The Witcher III: Wild Hunt and a fourth more broadly focused ‘General Skills' Skill Tree. The Skill Trees include:

- **Combat Skills –** This Skill Tree is dedicated to improving Geralt's melee and ranged combat by adding additional maneuvers and increasing damage. It also helps his general hardiness in combat with abilities to increase armor and health. Abilities in this Skill Tree will provide a passive bonus towards Adrenaline Point gain.
- **Signs –** The Signs Skill Tree focuses on signs and investing points in here will increase the effectiveness, improve duration or add additional functionality to each of Geralt's five signs: **AARD, AXII, IGNI, QUEN** and **YRDEN**. Abilities in this Skill Tree will provide a passive bonus towards Stamina regeneration in combat.
- **Alchemy –** The Alchemy Skill Tree focuses on improving the effectiveness of Bombs, Potions and Oils. It also features abilities to reduce the Toxicity levels of Potions and allows Geralt increased Toxicity tolerance, whilst also allowing him to reduce Toxicity quicker. Abilities in this Skill Tree will provide a passive bonus towards Potion Duration time.
- **General Skills –** The General Skill Tree is half the size of the other three Skill Trees and offers an assortment of useful abilities. These include boosts to Health/Armour, a minor health regeneration perk as well as bonuses to the crossbow and Adrenaline generation.

### Mutagens

Mutagens are a special form of equippable item that is dropped by monsters you kill. There are three main levels of Mutagens:

- **Lesser Mutagens –** These are dropped by the standard, common monster enemies.
- **Mutagens –** These can be crafted using Alchemy by combining three Mutagens of a specific type.
- **Greater Mutagens -** Dropped by more unique creatures or challenging boss monsters (almost all Witcher Contracts will net you specific Greater Mutagen types).

Mutagens can be used in a couple of different ways, either by providing passive bonuses to increase Geralts base stats for particular elements of gameplay, or as the core ingredient in the game's powerful Decoctions.

#### Mutagens for Passive Bonuses

In the Character Development section of the main menu, in addition to Skill Trees to spend your hard-earned Skill Points on, you can also apply Mutagens to Geralt for some passive bonuses.

Mutagens come in three different colours, each of which is associated with one of Geralt's more common gameplay attributes. Equipped Mutagens will provide a bonus towards the attribute which will increase with the level of Mutagen used, essentially the bonuses for Greater Mutagens are larger than those of Lesser Mutagens. The colours of Mutagens and their effects when equipped include:

- **Red Mutagens** increase attack power by +5% (Lesser), +7% (Normal) or +10% (Greater).
- **Green Mutagens** increase Vitality by +50 (Lesser), +100 (Normal) or +150 (Greater).
- **Blue Mutagens** increase Sign intensity by +5% (Lesser), +7% (Normal) or +10% (Greater).

There are four Mutagen slots in total with one unlocking after every seven character levels. The fourth and final slot is unlocked once Geralt reaches character level 28.

> **Note:** Inserting a Mutagen into all four slots will unlock the achievement/trophy **‘Mutant'**.

#### Mutagens for Crafting Decoctions

The Witcher III: Wild Hunt's Decoctions are very powerful versions of potions with much more potent effects. Their recipes often call for specific Greater **Mutagens**, which will only drop from specific unique or powerful monsters (usually from bosses or Witcher Contracts) once they have been killed.

### Evil's Soft First Touches

This quest will show up in your quest log as a main quest as soon as you have installed the new ‘Hearts of Stone' expansion.

The quest requires that we ride over to the Seven Cat's Inn to the east of Novigrad and visit the Notice Board highlighted by the objective marker. This will trigger a scene and a conversation after which your objectives will be updated.

#### Part 1: The Bounty

Ride over to the fresh objective marker located at the ‘Garin Estate' in the northeast corner of the world map. Enter the estate grounds and as you arrive at the manor a scene will play. During the conversation you'll be presented with a pair of options:

- **“Bunch of idiots”** – This will start a fight in which you'll need to beat up the group to continue.
- **“Amusing, but I'm here on business”**\- You'll be permitted to continue.

After the scene, follow the Wild One up the stairs and go through the door to speak with Olgierd about the contract. After speaking about the contract, you'll be given the opportunity to accept it. Do so to update your objectives.

> **Note:** For accepting the Witcher Contract you'll earn **150XP**.

Upon exiting the manor, you'll be engaged in conversation by a woman just outside of the door who'll hand you a new story quest.

> **Note:** The **‘Rose on a Red Field'** side-quest is now available.

To continue with **‘Evil's Soft First Touches'** we'll need to make our way to Oxenfurt. Head over to the objective marker here to find a ladder leading down a well into the sewers below. Climb down.

When you reach the floor, follow the passage a short distance to the right and you'll find a chest.

Return to the ladder and bang on your Witcher Sense. Examine the **trail of footprints** on the floor and follow them into the next room. You'll encounter a Drowner in here. Eliminate it and then use Witcher Sense to examine the other **Drowner corpse** here. Loot the chest in the alcove on the right-hand wall.

Continue through the gate and move forward for a scene.

When you are free to move again, move up to the warped grating in front and use Witcher Sense to detect a section of weak wall to the right. Use **AARD** to knock it down. Go through the new entrance and approach the yellow search area on the mini-map.

After another brief chat, drop down the hole in the floor. There is a group of six Drowners lurking about down here. Fight them all off for another scene and a chat during which we should examine the body to learn more about the monster lurking in the sewers.

After the conversation has run its course, another pair of Drowners will attack from an adjacent room. Kill them before proceeding through the large gate at the southern end of the room. Another pair of Drowners await in the room beyond the gate. Check out the dead end to the south for a chest and proceed through the door to the right of the gate to continue.

Proceed through this tunnel until you reach a larger, open room. A scene will play and after it comes to an end, you'll find yourself in the monster's lair. A boss fight will commence.

> ##### Boss: Toad “Prince”
>
> The Toad prince is a tough fight as not only does the boss have a large pool of health that you'll need to whittle away, but it also has a large number of damaging abilities which make it a tricky opponent.
>
> The Toad Prince favors his tongue for attacks – he can use a long-range tongue poke as well as a short range horizontal tongue swipe if you get in close. The boss will often hop in the air if you get to close to him. If you stay put and he lands nearby, Geralt will take damage and be knocked back briefly. The Toad can also spit between one and three green projectiles which can throw gunk up onto your screen to obscure your view of the fight if you take a hit from one. These projectiles when they land, will also produce a poisonous gas which can cause damage over time if you step in them.
>
> As the Toad's poison projectiles and their gas is nasty, you are going to want to try and stay mid-range to the boss so that you can stay out of the gas, but also be able to read and avoid his tongue attacks. The best tactic is to run in, hit him once or twice and then roll backwards as the boss will almost certainly jump after taking damage so you'll want to avoid the knockback effect that comes with his landing.

Upon defeating the boss, a scene will play.

> **Note:** For defeating the boss, you'll earn **600XP.**

> **Note:** For defeating the Toad Prince, you'll earn the **Achievement/Trophy ‘I'm Not kissing That'**.

#### Part 2: The Ofieri

After defeating the Toad Prince and a lengthy scene and conversation aboard a ship, said ship will sink. We'll be back in control of Geralt on a beach surrounded by Ofieri soldiers and a mage. You guessed it, it's another boss fight.

> ##### Boss: Ofieri mage
>
> This fight is fortunately, much easier that the Toad Price, even though you are severely outnumbered. There will be a group of six Ofieri soldiers armed with a combination of sabers and polearms along with a fellow bearing a shield and the Mage himself who will stay at a distance and fire of magical attacks at you from range.
>
> The Mage has a few moves at his disposal including a sand wave attack, which will usually be fired in a straight line from the caster directly in front and at Geralt. He also commonly utilizes a circular area of effect attack during which you'll see a circle of sand appear and shrink quickly. When it reaches the centre, it will explode, causing area of effect damage you are standing too closely. The Mage can also transform into a tornado and follow you around for a brief time, remaining invulnerable to attack and damaging Geralt if you attempt to attack. He can also perform a short-range teleport by sinking into the ground and re-emerging elsewhere. The Mage, despite his age is also no slouch when it comes to melee combat with his staff as he'll be able to rattle off significant combos which can be tricky to avoid if you get hit.
>
> The best tactic during this fight is to keep an eye on the Mage from a distance and avoiding his attacks when you see them coming, whilst first eliminating the melee enemies. AARD and QUEN are useful here for pushing back enemies if things get a little crowded or for some additional armor to guard against attacks from both the melee enemies as well as the Mage.
>
> After dispatching the melee enemies, it's time to focus on the Mage. With the Mage, it's a good idea to keep an eye on the sand that appears as most of his magic will be telegraphed by a little sand movement. Try to close the gap to him as quickly as possible to stay at close range to take his magic out of the equation. Hit him a couple of times and when he teleports away, again acoid his magic as you approach before engaging him in melee combat. Continue to do this and keep an eye out for the tornado attack – if you see it, stop what you are doing and run, you can't damage him and he will wreck Geralt if it hits him.
>
> Keep up the same chase down and melee attack tactics until the Mage falls.

Once you have damaged the Mage enough, a scene will play to end the fight.

> **Note:** For defeating the Ofieri Mage, you'll earn **200XP.**

#### Part 3: Meet Gaunter O'Dimm

With the Mage dealt with, we'll be free to head off once again. Loot the chest on the beach and the remnants of the ship before calling Roach. We'll now need to make our way to the town of Yantra to the east.

When you arrive in Yantra, locate the objective marker here. We'll need to meet our buddy Gaunter O'Dimm here at midnight, so if needed meditate to the appointed hour. When you are at the right spot at the right time, a scene will play and you'll learn a little bit more about what we'll be doing for the remainder of the expansion pack.

Agree to help Gaunter O'Dimm with his problem to progress with the quest.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **250XP**.

#### Part 4: Return to Ogierd Von Everec

At this point, we'll need to return to the **‘Garin Estate'** fast travel marker and head into the estate proper to catch up with Olgierd once again. Upon arrival at the estate however, you'll find the place is ablaze.

Approach the burning building and a scene/conversation will ensue. The second conversation options will change the next part of the quest somewhat.

- **“No need to be edgy.”** – The manor lord will die.
- **“Let him go, or face my wrath.”** – You'll need to fight a boss but the manor lord will live.
- **“Do what you will.”** – The manor lord will die.

If you choose either of the first or third options, the conversation will continue and the quest will progress through dialogue. If you choose the second option however, a boss fight will ensue.

> ##### Boss: Olgierd Von Everec
>
> Olgierd is a tough customer to come up against and uses that sword of his to great effect. He'll move slowly around the boss arena, but can move lightning fast when it comes to attacking so you'll need to approach him carefully.
>
> Approaching him front on will have him utilizing a series of attacks that can be chained together and can easily wipe out your entire health bar with a few hits if they land on Geralt. Staying from mid to long range from him will see Olgierd primarily use a teleport lunge attack which he'll link in with a short horizontal sword combo. He can also infuse his blade with glowing red magic and perform a lunging attack for massive damage, so if you see the sword start glowing, be prepared to dodge.
>
> In general, he'll fight like many of the other heavily armed humanoid bosses have previously. You'll need to whittle down his health whilst avoiding his melee attacks and maintaining awareness on his location when he teleports to prevent taking an attack from an unexpected angle.
>
> The boss is fast enough to avoid all of your offensive signs ( **IGNI, AARD, AXII**) and is impervious to the effects of **YRDEN**. As such, you'll need to focus on attacking with your sword when you spot openings in his attacks (Waiting for a melee attack or lunge, dodging and then quickly following up with a melee attack of your own seems to be the best method here). Whilst doing this, it's advisable to keep **QUEN** active as much as possible to maintain a shield whenever you can.

Once you have dealt enough damage to Olgierd, a scene will play to end the fight.

> **Note:** For completing the Witcher Contract you'll earn **325XP and 450XP.**

Continue the conversation that occurs, the choices here don't seem to matter as you'll end up in the same place at the end. After one final chat with Gaunter O'Dimm, you'll receive the Vone Everec Blood quest item. Once the conversation is completed, the quest will be completed.

Upon completion of the quest, you'll be offered two additional quests to continue with the story – **‘Dead Man's Party'** and **‘Open Sesame!'**. I've opted to do the **‘Dead Man's Party'** quest first in this guide as that was the quest that is automatically tracked when you receive them, although you can do the other if you so choose.

### Dead Man's Party

This quest will automatically be added to your quest log upon completing the ‘Evil's Soft First Touches' quest. To get started with it, we'll need to travel to Oxenfurt.

#### Part 1: Meet Shani

Once in Oxenfurt, make your way to Shani's Clinic which can be found roughly halfway between the ‘Oxenfurt Harbour' and ‘West Gate' fast travel markers. Upon arrival, enter the building and on the second floor you'll find Shani.

Speak with the guards first (dialogue options don't seem to change the outcome here, so feel free to choose what you like) before chatting with Shani herself to ask for her help. She'll let you know what you need to do next.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

#### Part 2: The Von Everec Crypt

After chatting with Shani, we'll next need to head to the Von Everec family crypt, located at the southern end of the Von Everc Estate itself far to the east of Novigrad. As you arrive at the objective marker, you'll find Shani waiting for you.

Approach her and a conversation will kick off. Again, dialogue options don't seem to matter here so respond as you wish. Once the conversation ends, she'll hand over a **Censer** and we'll be ready to proceed.

Follow Shani through the first gate and she'll lead you to the crypt entrance.

Enter the crypt and follow the stairs all the way to the bottom to reach a small, circular chamber. As you enter, a series of Wraiths will spawn and attack. There are four of these in total, but they will only spawn in a staggered manner, meaning you'll only ever face two of them at a time. Beware of their teleportation move as 90% of the time, they will appear immediately behind you and attack, so dodge and give them an attack back.

With the Wraiths dealt with, we'll need to explore the crypt. We can use Geralt's Witcher Sense to examine a few items of interest including:

- The **central altar** in the circular room.
- Three **Sarcophagi** in the hallway to the left.
- Three **Sarcophagi** in the central, circular room.
- Blow down the weak wall in the circular room to find a **corpse** holding the **Crypt Key**.
- The **Sword** placed before the right-hand sarcophagi in the central room.

Once you have located Vlodimir's resting place, our objectives will be updated. At this point, we'll now need to Use the **Censer** that Shani gave us. Be sure to equip it as a quick item so that you can activate it. When you are ready to go, you'll need to use the **Censer** in the following locations:

- By the pedestal in the centre of the circular room.
- At three points in the side room with the three sarcophagi to the left on the circular room.
- At the end of the side tunnel on the eastern side of the circular room.

Once all of the rooms have been filled with smoke from the **Censer**, return to the central, circular room and approach the votive (the central altar) and use your **IGNI** to light a fire. This will trigger a scene.

When the scene ends, you'll find yourself in combat against five Ghosts armed with melee weapons and polearms. They act in a similar manner to the packs of bandits scattered around in the wilderness whom you would have encountered by now. They can be pushed back with **AARD** (useful when you get cornered) and stunned with **AXII** but should fall pretty quickly against your swords.

After defeating the Von Everec Ghosts, Vlodimir himself will grace you with his presence. After a brief conversation our objectives will update.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **100XP**.

Before leaving the crypt, if you haven't already, blow down the weak wall in the central room and loot the corpse for the **Crypt Key.** Follow the cave here a short distance to come across some lootables (often containing crafting Diagrams, so are worth checking out). Further into this cave you'll find yourself in a web-covered area occupied by four Arachnomorphs. Clear them out and continue through the far end of the cave to reach another area with some sarcophagi waiting to be looted. Do so and use the **Crypt Key** on the gate to return to the main staircase leading to the exit.

## Campaign Walkthrough (Continued)

#### Part 3: The Wedding

Now that Vlodimir is able to possess Geralt from time to time we'll now need to take him to a wedding celebration. To do so, exit the crypt and make for the farm to the northeast indicated by the objective marker. As you arrive, a scene will play.

> **Note:** During the next section of this quest, Vlodimir will attempt to hit on and woo Shani at every chance he gets. Fortunately, you'll have a choice of dialogue options each time he does so. As it is possible that coming on too strongly may impact on your ability to romance Shani later on, its recommended that you try to choose the most respectable answer each time.

Following the scene/conversation, we'll be inside the wedding and our objectives will update.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **100XP**.

Stay with Shani and she'll introduce lead you over to the newlyweds. Speak with them. After the brief chat, you'll be free to wander around the wedding. Here you can speak, drink and undertake a number of different activities to while away the time.

> **Note:** There is an **achievement/trophy ‘Let the Good Times Roll!'** at this point in time, which we can earn for taking part in all of the available activities at the wedding.

There are four activities that are required to progress the quest, but 10 in total. As this is the case, if you want to earn the **‘Let the Good Times Roll!' achievement/trophy** then it's best to complete the optional activities as soon as possible as some of them will become inaccessible after certain required activities. The optional activities at the wedding include the following:

1. Locate the gate that we entered from and just to its right you'll see a table with some drinks. Approach and interact with the drinks to complete the first optional activity.
2. From the table with the drinks, climb the wooden stairs and follow the path at the top. Speak with the woman here and Vlodimir will flirt with her to complete the second optional activity..
3. Climb down the small stone stairs to find an entrance to the barn. Instead of entering, climb the ladder to the left of the door. In the room at the top, sit on the pillow indicated by the objective marker to complete the third optional activity.
4. Return down the ladder and enter the barn. Find the stage with people dancing on and in front of it. Stand in the objective marker here and Geralt will begin to dance after a short while. This will complete the fourth optional activity.
5. Also inside the barn is a table marked with an objective marker, interact with the drinks on the table to complete the fifth optional activity.
6. Exit the barn and speak with the woman beneath the large tree on the eastern side of the farm and Vlodimir will flirt with her as well. Doing so will complete the sixth optional activity.

With all of the optional wedding activities out of the way, we can now focus on the more important activities. Most of these require a little more effort than those listed above, but are no less difficult. Note that between each of the activities, you'll have a brief conversation with ShaniThey include the following:

##### Activity 1: The Pig Pen

At the pig pen on the eastern side of the farm and speak with the onlookers. We'll need to chase a pair of pigs marked with golden circles on their backs into the pens. The easiest way to do so is to simply use AXII. Doing so successfully will reward you with the King of Swineherd's Crown Trophy.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **40XP**.

After the first activity, Shani will encourage Vlodimir to try his flirting techniques on a woman nearby. Choose any conversation option you wish. No matter what you say during this conversation, it will end in a fight against three opponents. Knock them all out to continue.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

##### Activity 2: Diving for Shoes

At the small dam on the southern side of the farm we'll again want to speak with the onlookers. We'll need to dive into the water and recover a shoe. Once underwater, use Geralt's Witcher Sense to locate the shoes at the bottom of the damn. There are three shoes in total to find. Once you have all three, return to Shani on the wooden platform overlooking the lake and speak with her to continue.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **40XP**.

After the second activity, another scene will play. During the conversation remember to use respectful responses to Shani when she speaks so that you have a shot with her romantically a little later on. During the timed decision that occurs choose whatever you feel right (I couldn't tell if there was a consequence to this decision outside of dialogue changes). When Geralt gets to speak with Shani, its best to choose the “Wouldn't force myself on you” or the “I'd probably have done exactly what he did” options.

> **Note:** Following the dance, Gaunter O'Dimm will appear in the barn and we can speak with him to progress the quest. Ignore him for the moment and instead head back outside to complete the remaining two activities.

##### Activity 3: Gwent

On the southern side of the wedding venue, you'll find a group of Halflings playing Gwent. Speak to them and play a game to complete the activity. Note that it doesn't matter if you win or lose the game, as long as you have played a game to completion the activity will count as completed. If you do lose the game, Geralt will receive the **Donkey's Ears** item.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **40XP**.

##### Activity 4: The Fire Breather

In the centre of the farm area, you'll find the Bride's parents. Approach them for a chat and you'll discover that they are having a problem. Agree to help them out.

Make your way to the dog's kennel in the southern portion of the wedding venue. Here, pop on Geralt's Witcher Senses and you'll be able to examine the following:

- The **kennel** and attached broken chain.
- The **gnawed bone** on the ground by the kennel.
- The **water dish** on the ground by the kennel.
- The **footprints** on the ground in front of the kennel.

Once you have examined the **footprints**, a more prominent set of them will appear nearby. Use Geralt's Witcher Sense to first examine these and then follow them out the gate and a short distance until you find the **Fire swallower's cap** on the ground. Keep following the footprints a little further and then the red ripple when you see it to find the fire swallower.

A scene will play. Choose to either scare the dog off or use **AXII** (you'll get **40XP** if you choose the latter).

Following the conversation with the Fire Breather, you'll need to escort him back to the wedding. This is mostly painless, however when you cross the stream you will be attacked by a Wild Boar. Keep it away from the Fire Breather and eliminate it for another scene to complete the activity.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

With all of the activities at the wedding now completed, make your way back into the barn and locate Gaunter O'Dimm. Speak with him to progress the quest.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **125XP**.

After chatting with Gaunter O'Dimm, we'll need to head a short distance into the barn nearby until you reach the search area on the mini-map. One final scene will play. After Vlodimir leaves the scene, but before ending the conversation, make sure that you choose the “I'd like to stay a bit longer” option to trigger the **‘A Midnight Clear'** side-mission.

Following the conversation, our objectives will be updated.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

> **Note:** At this point, if you completed all 10 activities at the wedding, you'll earn the achievement/trophy **‘Let the Good Times Roll!'**.

At this point, it is best to complete the **‘A Midnight Clear'** side-quest as it will fail if you leave the wedding.

#### Part 4: A Midnight Clear Side-quest (Optional)

Whilst we are still at the wedding, we now have the opportunity to undertake one of the expansions side-quests ‘A Midnight Clear'. This brief side-quest will give Geralt an opportunity for romance with Shandi before proceeding with the expansion. The side-quest will fail if we leave the wedding venue so if you want to do it, now is the time!

After Gaunter O'Dimm's advice, we'll need to find a gift for Shandi. To do so, check the character entry for Shandi in the glossary portion of the main menu. Afterwards, use Geralt's Witcher Senses to explore the three search areas nearby to find something she'll enjoy.

- **Rowan flowers** on the tree by the quest start location - this is a good choice!
- **Bottle of Mead** on the table closest to the barn entrance - this is not such a good choice.
- **Rowanberry Brandy** on a table inside the barn - this is a good choice!

> **Note:** For picking one of the three items up, you'll earn **125XP**.

With your gift in hand, make your way into the barn and find Shandi using the objective marker on the mini-map. Approach her and initiate a conversation to continue. During the conversation, be sure to choose the **”You've got me.”** option to increase your chances of success.

After leaving the wedding, you'll have to complete a couple of quick-time decisions. The first has the following options, neither of which seem to affect the outcome in any way:

- **“Weddings not my thing.”**
- **“It wasn't bad.“**

During the next section of dialogue, choose the **“Unfit? What's that mean?”** option. Soon after, you'll come across another timed decision, which also has a number of options available. I'd choose either the first or third options though for the best outcome.

- **“I prefer you.”**
- **“Would've survived somehow.”**
- **“That was** Vlodimir **, not me.”**

Following the decision, choose the **“Better by a mile.”** Dialogue option before finally, during the final timed decision ensure that you choose the **“Kiss sure didn't sate me”.** This will trigger the romance scene between Shani and Geralt.

> **Note:** Depending on the gift that you gave her, the scene will play out slightly differently. Drinking all of the alcohol will result in Shani feeling a little under the weather around halfway through.

Following the scene, you'll awaken on the shore of the lake nearby the next morning. Speak with Shani one more time here to complete the side-quest.

> **Note:** For completing the side-quest you'll earn **125XP**.

#### Part 5: Return to Olgierd

With the wedding now completed and Vlodimir's spirit happier than it was earlier, return to Olgierd at the Alchemist Inn in Oxenfurt to hand in and complete the quest.

During the conversation, if you have completed the **‘Open Sesame!'** quest already, Olgierd will fill you in on your next assignment **‘Scenes From a Marriage'**. If not, it's time to push onward with **‘Open Sesame!'**.

> **Note:** For completing the quest you'll earn **125XP**.

### Open Sesame! - Part 1

This quest will automatically be added to your quest log upon completing the ‘Evil's Soft First Touches' quest. To get started with it, we'll need to travel to Oxenfurt.

#### Part 1: The Auction House

Once you are in Oxenfurt, head to the Bordosi auction house which is indicated by the objective marker just to the north of the main market area. As you approach you'll be stopped by guards at the entrance. Progress through the dialogue as you wish as one of Geralt's mates will show up and invite him inside.

After another short chat, you'll be back in control of Geralt and inside the auction house.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **100XP**.

Make your way up the stairs whilst following Vimme and when you reach the top, he'll will point out three persons of interest that you can speak with whilst you are waiting for the auction to begin. Although this is an optional objective, it's definitely worth speaking with them. You'll be able to converse with the following people:

##### Countess Mignole

The countess can be found by one of the windows. She is a merchant who will sell you the four pieces of Viper School Witcher armor ( **Diagram: Viper Armor**, **Diagram: Viper Boots**, **Diagram: Viper Gauntlets** and **Diagram: Viper Trousers**) included in the expansion, so it's a good idea to snaffle these form her right away as you won't have another opportunity.

Inquire as to what she might be interested in at the auction and she'll give you a short side-quest. Move out to the open balcony and use Geralt's Witcher Senses to examine a **Wine Stain**. This will trigger another brief conversation with the countess.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **100XP**.

##### Hilbert

Hilbert is located leaning on the buffet table just before the open balcony. You can make small talk with him but he doesn't have a great deal of interest to say. You can however play Gwent with him if you like to pass the time a little.

##### Yaromir

The final guest, Yaromir can be found by the paintings opposite the buffet table. After a brief chat, he'll question your knowledge of the art world. Guess the correct painting when he quizzes you it's the portrait of the merchant and he'll give you a tip on the upcoming auction. Do not tell Vimme about the tip though, or he'll attempt to outbid you and you'll end up paying significantly more for it.

> **Note:** After chatting with all three of Vimme's friends, you'll earn **100XP**.

#### Part 2: The Auction

After chatting with all of Vimme's friends, a scene will play and the auction will begin pretty much immediately afterwards.

> **Note:** There is an **achievement/trophy ‘Shopaholic'** which can be unlocked by purchasing all three items up for auction.

During the auction, there is an opportunity to receive the missable follow up side-quest ‘Avid Collector' as well as unlocking the achievement/trophy ‘Shopaholic'. To get both of these in a single sitting, you'll need to outbid all of the other NPCs and purchase all three of items that are up for offer. Fortunately, having around 1,000 Crowns should be sufficient to purchase all three. These items include:

- **Count Romilly's Figurine** – 300 Crowns
- **Painting: Starry Night Over the Pontar** – 20 Crowns (80 Crowns if you told Vimme about the tip)
- **The Professor's Spectacles** – 350 Crowns

> **Note:** After purchasing all three of these items, the **‘Shopaholic' achievement/trophy** will unlock.

In addition to the **‘Shopaholic' achievement/trophy**, at this point you'll also unlock a new side-quest **‘Avid Collector'**.

Following the auction, you'll get your opportunity to speak with Horst Borsodi. Unfortunately, he's not too interested in talking with you and no matter what is said, Geralt will find himself kicked outside of the auction house without any way back in again.

As soon as you regain control, you'll be in a fistfight against a pair of Guards. Knock them both out to continue. Another scene will play.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **200XP**.

#### Part 3: The Herbalist's Hut

When you regain control once more, we'll be tasked with heading over to the Herbalist's hut just to the northeast of Oxenfurt.

Make your way to the objective marker and as you approach a scene will play. When you regain control, Geralt will be on his feet and being attacked by a Wild Boar. Fend this off before continuing up the road towards the Herbalist's Hut.

At the entrance to the hut, you'll come across a group of three Fallen Knights. Two of these fellows have shields whilst the third is armed with a war hammer. To make the shielded enemies much more manageable, use AARD to knock them back and open up their defenses.

Once all three enemies have been defeated, the door to the hut will open and the herbalist will come out to chat with you. During the conversation, repeat the password **“Need some yarrow extract.”** to him and he'll open a trapdoor for you at the back of his shop.

Climb down the ladder to reach the basement. Continue inside and a scene will play.

Following the conversation, the quest will now continue tracking in the background, but will also trigger three additional quests which feed into it **‘Open Sesame: Witcher Seasonings'**, **‘Open Sesame: Breaking and Entering'** and **‘Open Sesame: The Safecracker'**. The quest will continue again, but only once you have finished all three of these quests.

Although you can attempt these quests in any order, as **‘Open Sesame: Witcher Seasonings'** can be undertaken right away, let's start with that one.

### Open Sesame: Witcher Seasonings

After speaking with the fellow in the Herbalist's hut, we'll receive this quest as part of the **‘Open Sesame!'** quest. This requires us to first create a **Cleansing Mixture**. Fortunately, Geralt will have whipped up the recipe on his onesies and if you enter the Alchemy section of the main menu and scroll down to the ‘Quests' header, you'll find it.

The Cleansing Mixture requires **1x Dwarven Spirit**, **2x Rotfiend Blood** and **2x Wolfsbane**. If you don't have these ingredients on hand, all of them can be purchased from the Herbalist in the main store area above where you received the quest.

With all the ingredients in hand, concoct the **Cleansing Mixture** to update your objectives.

Now that the **Cleansing Mixture** has been crafted, we'll need to make our way over to Oxenfurt and eavesdrop on a series of guards to try and find the information that we're interested in. For each, you'll need to approach the objective marker on the map and use Geralt's Witcher Sense from a distance (but within the quest area marker) to eavesdrop on the conversation that the pair of guards are having. There are three sets of guards and they can be found in the following locations:

- In front of a building located at the northern end of the market area.
- The rear (northern side) of the building that the first pair of guards were guarding. We can listen to a pair of guards through an open window.
- Just along the riverfront path to the east of the second group. This pair of guards will walk, so you'll need to follow at a safe distance whilst eavesdropping.

After listening to all three sets of guards, your objectives will update. A new objective marker will appear at the end of a pier on the western side of Oxenfurt just west of the ‘Western Gate' fast travel marker.

At the end of the dock here is a fisherman named Maarten. He'll only appear here during the day, so be sure to rest until he's there. Approach and speak with him to get him to help you. You can choose either of the following dialogue options to convince him:

- **“Fine. Willing to Pay.”** – Pay the cook 500 Crowns.
- **“You'll help me free of charge.”** \- Geralt will use **AXII**(requires level 2 Delusion) and he'll do it for free. You'll also earn **40XP**.

Either choice will result in the quest being completed.

> **Note:** At this point you'll earn **125XP**.

### Open Sesame: The Safecracker

After speaking with the fellow in the Herbalist's hut, we'll receive this quest as part of the ‘Open Sesame!' quest. This quest will give us two different objective markers on the map, requiring that we undertake a pair of tasks to recruit either Quinto or Casimir in order to complete it. Depending on your choice, the subsequent mission will play out in a slightly different way. You'll need to choose at this point:

- **[Option 01 – Quinto](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Option%2001%20-%20Quinto).** Quinto is an expert lock and will provide you with a stealthier option during the heist later on.
- **[Option 02 – Casimir.](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Option%2002%20-%20Casimir)** Casimir is a demolitions fellow offering a decidedly less subtle, explosive method to use during the heist later on, resulting in additional guard encounters.

##### Option 01 - Quinto

Quinto's location is closer as he's being held in a mercenary camp just to the southeast of the herbalist's hut. Make your way to the mercenary camp and you'll discover the NPC of interest locked in one of the small cages. Approach and speak with him to learn about his predicament.

Nearby is a mercenary leader named Hans. Speaking with him will give you numerous conversation options to proceed. We can choose between the following:

- **“Let's make a deal.”**\- You'll be able to negotiate the asking price down to $175 or so. Pay this sum to Hans and he'll release Quinto.
- **“Fine. I'll pay.”** – Pay Hans 200 Crowns. He'll release Quinto.
- **“How about a game of Gwent.”** – Defeating Hans in a game of Gwent will have him hand over Quinto.
- **“You're acting like an old fishwife.”** This will result in a fight during which you'll have to best a group of six mercenaries. Approach and open the cage afterwards.

Either way you go about freeing Quinto, a scene will play during which you can tell him to head back to the herbalist's hut to complete the quest.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

##### Option 02 - Casimir

Of the two options, Casimir is located further away. He can be found in the village of Alness, which is along the road just south of the **‘Dancing Windmill'** fast travel marker to the east of Novigrad. He'll be sitting on a rooftop with a crowd of NPCs looking up at him.

Approach the building Casimir is sitting upon and a conversation will ensue. Offer to speak with him. We essentially need to talk him down from blowing himself up and to do so we can try to talk some sense into him, but truthfully, by far the safest and easiest way to do this is to simply use **AXII**(level 2 Delusion) on him to end the conversation as soon as it starts. This will also net you **40XP**.

The good news is that if you do try and negotiate with him and he does go and blow himself to smithereens, the quest will not fail as it is still possible to recruit Quinto.

After liberating the dwarf from his rooftop conundrum, a scene will play during which you can tell him to head back to the herbalist's hut to complete the quest.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **150XP**.

### Open Sesame: Breaking and Entering

After speaking with the fellow in the Herbalist's hut, we'll receive this quest as part of the ‘Open Sesame!' quest. This quest will give us two different objective markers on the map for recruiting both Hugo Hoff and Eveline Gatto to join your crew. Note that if you are hungry for some XP, it is possible to do both objectives as long as you head to Hugo Hoff's location first.

#### Part 1: Hugo Hoff

Hugo Hoff's safe house can be found in the eastern portion of Oxenfurt. Approach the objective marker and interact with the door for a brief scene. Following the scene, activate your Witcher Sense to explore the area. You'll be able to inspect the following:

- The **blood** spatter on the beam opposite the doorway.

After examining the blood stain, you'll be able to find a **set of footprints** on the ground nearby leading away from the door. Follow the footprints until they end on the beach in the southwest corner of Oxenfurt. Here you'll encounter a group of three thugs.

After dispatching the three thugs loot their corpses. One will be holding a letter – **Soaked Letter** and **Hugo Hoff's Ear**. Acquiring both these items will update your objectives.

Swim out to the search area in the water to the west and use Geralt's Witcher Sense to locate the body underwater before examining it. Afterwards, swim back to shore to complete the first part of the quest. Our objectives will update.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **50XP**.

#### Part 2: Eveline Gatto

The second person of interest, Eveline Gatto can be found chatting with another fellow named Meryn at a circus camp located to the east of the ‘Vegelbud Residence' and just northeast of the ‘Carsten' fast travel markers. Approach and speak with Eveline to continue.

Unfortunately, she's not going to help you without you helping her out first. Agree to do so and you'll find yourself armed with a crossbow. Simply shoot the four apples off of the other performer nearby to complete Eveline's task.

After completing the task, a scene will play during which you can tell her to head back to the herbalist's hut to complete the quest.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **125XP**.

### Open Sesame! - Part 2

After completing the three sub-quests **‘Open Sesame: Witcher Seasonings'**, **‘Open Sesame: Breaking and Entering'** and **‘Open Sesame: The Safecracker'** you should have recruited all of the persons of interest required to undertake the heist and this quest can therefor continue once again.

When you are ready to progress, make your way back to the Herbalist's hut and head down to the basement. Approach the crew down here for a scene to trigger the next portion of the quest and to update your objectives.

Following the conversation, the heist can begin. Either choose the option to fast travel with the rest of the crew to the rendezvous point in Oxenfurt, or make your own way there. They can be found in a small alleyway just to the left of the entrance to the auction house at the northern end of the market area and will appear at 12AM sharp, so meditate until the required time if necessary.

When you do meet up with them, a scene will play.

> **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **80XP**.

#### Part 3: The Heist

Following the scene, you'll be in the attic of the auction house building. If you use Geralt's Witcher Sense, you can find a collection of **Love Letters** on a crate. There is also a chest to loot near the initial starting point.

Make your way down the stairs to the lower level and towards the objective marker. As you approach a scene will play. There will be numerous guards on the lower floor and we'll need to fight a group of five of them. Fortunately, your allies will help you out so it's not as tricky a prospect as it could have been. After defeating enough guards (2-3 of them) another scene will play.

After fighting off the Guards our crew will decide that the best way to deal with the situation at hand is to take hostages. A brief timed decision will flash up on the screen. I'd advise you to choose the **“Pull back from the auction house”** option initially though as having hostages will make this next part easier (and as far as I can tell, nothing bad will happen to them either so don't be afraid of accidently killing anyone as long as you follow my instructions below).

Depending on your choice of safecracker, the next part of the quest will play out slightly differently. Your options are:

- **[Option 01 - Quinto](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Option%2001%20%E2%80%93%20Quinto)**
- **[Option 02 - Casimir](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Option%2002%20%E2%80%93%20Casimir)**

##### Option 01 – Quinto

During the negotiations with the guardsman outside and with Quinto as your safecracker you'll have the option to avoid fighting them altogether. To do so, you'll need to choose the following options during the timed decisions that appear:

- **“Pull back from the auction house”**
- **“Bring us a wagon and horses”**
- **“Doesn't matter. Long as it rolls.”**
- **“I'll set one free now. The rest later.”**

> **Note:** If you stray from these responses, the guards will lose their patience and attack. The good news is that there are only three of them, so it's not a difficult fight by any means.

### Scenes From a Marriage

After acquiring this quest from Olgierd, to start it you'll need to venture back to the Von Everec Estate to the east of Oxenfurt. Approach the objective marker at the gate to the estate to discover that it is locked.

Follow the wall to the left of the gate a short distance until you encounter an NPC looking for a friend. After a brief chat, you'll find yourself within the walls.

#### Part 1: Explore the manor

To begin with, we need to move through the estate's courtyard and examine each of the yellow search areas using Witcher Sense. You'll be able to inspect the following:

- **Bags of manure** in the closest search area to the entrance.
- A **fountain**, a **bench** and a **grave** in the search area just past the bags of manure. There is a Black Dog next to the grave that you can speak to.
- An **easel** at the top of the stairs to the left of the locked gate (whilst facing the gate).
- The **series of beehives** on the western side of the courtyard.
- The **broken lamp** on the ground by the door to the main building.

Once you have examined the **broken lamp**, drag marks and **footprints** will appear nearby. Use Geralt's Witcher Sense to first examine these and then follow them as they lead you around to a gate leading to the rear of the manor.

Opening the gate will trigger a scene. When you regain control, move over to where the Black Cat was initially sitting to find some **Lockpicks**. Follow the footprints and the digging noises into the back courtyard further until another cut-scene plays. This will also trigger a boss fight.

> ##### Boss: The Caretaker
>
> The Caretaker is armed with a heavy two-handed axe and will swing it in wide arcs in front of him usually 1-2 times but can chain these together into a longer series of relatively quick strikes. He also infuses his weapon with magic before making use of an overhead slam attack which will cause bones to rise from the ground surrounding its impact point inflicting area of effect damage. This overhead attack will have his axe get stuck in the ground briefly, but after a few moments he'll pull it out, again triggering an area of effect attack around the weapon's location.
>
> His attacks will do a significant amount of damage and if they hit you, he'll also regain some of his health back based upon the damage dealt.
>
> The Caretaker is a Relict and as such is weak against **Relict Oil**. As you might expect, attempting to dodge and attack during his quick strike phase is a little silly when you can simply lure him into performing his magic overhead strike and wail on him for a few moments whilst he is immobilized trying to free his weapon. Don't be greedy though – hit him 2-3 times before backing off to avoid the area effect damage that occurs as he pulls the axe from the ground. Its best to use **QUEN** to increase your time to attack the boss at this point and reduce the damage cause by the area effect attacks if you get stuck in them.
>
> After reducing his health to around 40%, he'll summon a cloud of fog that will shroud the ground and zombies will rise all around the area. Not only will they attack you, but if the Caretaker kills them, he'll also regain some health back. At this point, you need to either wipe out all the zombies as quickly as possible, or alternatively rush in and attack the Caretaker head-on, killing only the zombies that get in your way. Reduce the boss's health as quickly as possible to prevent a drawn-out fight.

After defeating the Caretaker a scene will play. You'll also receive the Key to the Manor's Back Door item.

> **Note:** For defeating the Caretaker you'll earn **50XP**.

Before heading for the back door, we can explore the area a little further with Witcher Sense to spy a few more points of interest. These include:

- **The Caretaker's Spade** – a new relic weapon located by the body of the Caretaker.
- An **Ornamented Brush** found beneath a dilapidated gazebo in the southeastern corner of the graveyard.
- **Bag of seeds and bulbs** in the eastern part of the graveyard by the fountain.
- Series of **fresh graves** in the eastern part of the graveyard by the fountain.
- **Half-destroyed Statue** to the right of the stairs leading up to where we saw the Caretaker initially.
- A **statue** on the western side of the graveyard.

When you are ready to continue, head over to the door at the rear of the manor marked by the objective marker and now flanked by the Black Dog and the Black Cat. Proceed inside.

Once inside, move through the open door on the left-hand side of the far wall. After a brief scene, loot the pair of chests on the right-hand wall (these contained Diagram **: Novigrad Longsword**, **Manuscript Page: Superior Necrophage Oil** and a **Mastercrafted Cavalry Saddle** for me) before grabbing **Iris' Sketchbook** from the small table opposite.

Continue through the open door to find a set of stairs. Before heading up, if you are interested, you can examine the right-hand wall for a portrait of Olgierd and the room opposite the one we came from for a set table. Climb the stairs to continue.

At the top of the stairs, inspect the portrait of Iris and Olgierd before going through only available open door. Exit to the balcony on the other side of this room and use it to access the adjacent room. Proceed through the door that spookily opens for you to start another boss fight.

> ##### Boss: The Wraith from the Painting
>
> The Wraith in the Von Everec manor is somewhat similar to the Wraiths you have fought in the game so far, but with some notable exceptions. The Wraith here has significantly more health and can do a lot more damage with her attacks. The she will perform the same general rapid melee swipes as a regular Wraith and will also use a screaming attack which will stun Geralt if he is too close to it.
>
> One thing that makes this fight trickier than most is that the Wraith can scream and cause paintings around the fight area to glow. These will transmit a beam of green energy which will link up with the boss and continue to regenerate her health until you can smack the painting with an attack of your own.
>
> As with other Wraiths, she can be slowed and have her physical form shown by **YRDEN**. After she has stepped through a **YRDEN** trap you'll be able to deal slightly more damage to her although in this fight, the effect is somewhat muted and definitely not as effective as usual. It's a much better idea to get in close and avoid her attacks whilst countering with your own and trying to keep a **QUEN** shield up as much as possible to mitigate any damage you may neglect to avoid. Just wait for an opening, attack and repeat!

After defeating the Wraith, the room will return to normal.

> **Note:** For defeating the Wraith from the Painting you'll earn **50XP**.

Continue through the now open door at the far end of the room. Inside you'll spot a corpse on the bed – be sure to loot the rest of the room for Portrait of Iris and Olgierd on a bench by the other door and the containers within if interested before examining the corpse. This is a really cool looking area which is a new version of the Von Everec manor which is located within one of Iris' paintings. > **Note:** At this point, you'll earn **50XP**.

### A Midnight Clear

**Start Location:** This side-quest can be started and completed towards the end of the ‘Dead Man's Party' story quest.

This brief side-quest will give Geralt an opportunity for romance with Shandi before proceeding with the expansion. The side-quest will fail if we leave the wedding venue so if you want to do it, now is the time!

Afterwards, use Geralt's Witcher Senses to explore the three search areas nearby to find something she'll enjoy.

- **Rowan flowers** on the tree by the quest start location - this is a good choice!
- **Bottle of Mead** on the table closest to the barn entrance - this is not such a good choice.

### Races: Swift as the Western Wind

**Start Location:** Speak to the Ofieri merchant at the **‘Upper Mill'** fast travel marker and challenge him to a race.

As with the races in the main game, this new horse race will play out in essentially the same way. You'll simply need to beat your opponent to the finish line whilst sticking as closely as you can to the racing line that appears on the mini-map. For horse racing, there are a few general tips that you should always look to exploit:

- Getting in front early is your best bet. Be sure alternate between canter and running to recharge your horse's stamina though!
- When you are in front, you can drop back from a gallop to a canter and effectively move back and forward to block other horses coming from behind. This is especially effective in narrow segments of track.
- Be sure to stay on the racing line! Some races will fail you if you stray too far and Roach is likely to come to a screeching halt if you hit any slight obstacle along the way.

The course here is fairly long as far as races go and it also gets narrow in places as you cross several bridges – so be sure to stay in the centre of the track to avoid having Roach coming into contact with the edges. At some points, there will also be barrels located or tossed onto the designated race track so make sure you jump over them when you see them to maintain speed. You'll have a single opponent in this race and as long as you use the tips above and avoid bumping into obstacles or careening off fences too frequently you should have no real issues with achieving victory.

> **Note:** For winning the race, you'll earn the Ofieri **Stock Saddle** and **100XP**.

### Hidden Treasure: The Cursed Chapel

> ##### Boss: Cursed Mother
>
> The Cursed Mother is quite similar to the Wraiths you have fought in the game so far, she just has significantly more health and can do a lot more damage with her attacks. The Cursed Mother will perform a lunging melee attack as her go-to move which will cause significantly more damage than a regular Wraith's swipe attacks. At several points during the fight the Cursed Mother will blind Geralt temporarily and create a pair of clones of herself which will restore her health as long as they are out. As such, when she splits into several clones kill them as quickly as possible to reduce the amount of health regain she is able to achieve.
>
> As with most Wraiths, the Cursed Mother is particularly susceptible to **YRDEN** traps which will pull her into a physical form. **YRDEN** is an absolute necessity as she will take next to no damage whilst in her spectral form. ## Gwent Guide

In addition to tonnes of narrative driven side-content and the lengthy main quest line, The Witcher III: Wild Hunt also includes a surprisingly deep, fun and addictive card game going by the name of Gwent. During your adventure, Geralt can find cards, build a deck, try his hand against Merchants and Innkeepers as well as hunting down and playing various top-tier players to cement his place as the best Gwent player in the history of the universe (or something).

You can first play Gwent upon arriving in the town of White Orchard. In the tavern, you'll want to speak with the Scholar at the table by the bar. He'll introduce the game to you and initiate a short tutorial on how to play before plunging you headlong into your first match.

This short section the guide is dedicated to outlining the basics of the game, giving you a few hints and tips as to how to win and include a list of every card in the game and where to find them all.

### How to Play

Gwent is played across three rounds with the winner being the first player to win two rounds. At the start of the match, players draw 10 cards from their deck. At this point, if you are unhappy with your cards there is the option to discard two cards from the hand to draw another two. Once the drawing is done, players must utilise the hand given across the three rounds of the game. As such, card and resource management is very important!

Essentially the Gwent Board is divided into three sections for each player in which they can play Infantry (Front), Ranged (Middle) and Siege (Back) unit cards. These unit cards each have an attack value attributed to them and the player with the highest total attack score at the end of each round is victorious.

Whilst it sounds simple enough, there's plenty of depth to the game. It's not a simple matter of placing attacking cards onto the board as there are numerous cards that have special abilities with a range of effects that can drastically impact what happens on the table. Additionally, there are four different decks available (Northern Realms, Monsters, Nilfgaardian Empire and Scoia'tael) and depending on the faction selected, there are certain special abilities granted to players which can further influence the flow of the game.

Players are able to pass/withdraw and sit out of a round at any time once they have placed their first card. When one player has withdrawn, the other can still play as many cards as they like until they too decide to withdraw. The scores are then tallied and the winner of the round determined.

### Card Types and Special Abilities

#### Card Types

Gwent features a range of different card types, each with their own unique uses and abilities. The following list outlines the various types of cards in the game and what makes each unique.

- **Unit Cards:** These are the basic attacking cards and will be the most abundant of the cards you come across. They come in three flavours – Infantry, Ranged and Siege.
- **Leader Cards:** Leader Cards are the boss card of your deck. They cannot be placed onto the table, but sit in the background. Each leader card has four variations, each which has a unique special ability that can be utilised once a game to help turn the tide in your favour.
- **Hero Cards:** Gwent's Hero Cards are the superheroes of the game and can be described as more powerful variants of your standard unit cards. They feature useful abilities such as being completely unaffected by weather effects and usually have a high attack scores. The Hero Cards are locked into specific factions, so if you have a deck set up for said faction, as you find these cards it's a good idea to incorporate them into your Gwent deck and replace your standard units as you go.
- **Neutral Cards:** Neutral cards are Hero cards that do not belong to a faction and therefore can be incorporated into a deck featuring any faction. As with heroes they are unaffected by weather cards and have a high attack value. In addition to being wildcards and being available for any deck, they also usually feature useful buffing abilities to give your hand an edge.
- **Weather Cards:**Whenplayedright,weather cards can be extremely effective at nullifying damage from opponents, the problem is weather cards, when played will affect both sides of the board. There are four types of weather cards, with three associated with messing with different lines of the Gwent board and reducing the attack power of units affected to 1. These include:
  - **Biting Frost –** This will reduce the attack values of all infantry cards to 1.
  - **Impenetrable Fog -** This will reduce the attack values of all Ranged cards to 1.
  - **Torrential Rain –** This will reduce the attack values of all Siege Engine cards to 1.
  - **Clear Weather -** Clears all weather effects from the board.
- **Special Cards:** Similar to the weather cards, these special cards are one-off use cards that can cause a number effects to the current cards in play. Using them in the right conditions at the right time and they can change the entire tone of a round.These special cards include **:**
  - **Commander's Horn –** Doubles the strength of units on a given row.
  - **Decoy –** Switch this with a card on the table to return it to your hand.
  - **Scorch –** Destroys the highest value card/s in play. Will effect both sides of the table.

#### Card Special Abilities

In addition to their Strength values, many unit cards also have their own unique abilities which can be used to your advantage. Below you'll find a list of the special abilities that unit cards can have and a short description on what they'll do and how they can be used. The special abilities include:

- **Agile:** The Agile ability is a handy one to have on your unit cards as those with this ability can be placed in either the front or middle row of the table, potentially avoiding weather effect cards already in play. The Agile ability is especially is abundant in the Scoia'tael deck and
- **Medic:** Playing a card with the Medic ability will allow you to look at your discarded cards and select a previously used unit card to play onto the board instantly. Using a Medic card to revive another with the Medic ability will have that trigger as well, effectively enabling players to revive multiple unit cards in a single turn.
- **Morale Boost:** A unit card with this ability will increase the Strength of every other unit card in the same row by 1. It will not increase its own strength.
- **Muster:** Playing a card with the Muster ability will search your deck with any other cards with the same name and place them onto the board, in play instantly.
- **Spy:** The Spy ability is one of the better ones that a unit card can possess. When played, the Spy card is placed on your opponent's side of the board (and will count towards their total strength pool) and will allow you to draw two additional cards.
- **Tight Bond:** When a unit card with Tight Bond is placed next to a unit card with the same name, the Tight Bond ability works by doubling the strength of both unit cards.

### Constructing a Deck

There are four different deck factions in Gwent and each has their own unique cards, special abilities and heroes. There are 150 cards in total in the game that can be acquired through numerous means including beating other players and purchasing them from merchants who have cards in stock. Upon acquiring new cards it's a good idea to have look at your stock and see what you can do about deck building – the starter deck will only get you so far!

There are four factions, each with their own unique special ability. They include:

- **Nilfgaardian Empire –** Will win a round if it ends in a draw.
- **Northern Realms –** Players are able to draw an additional card upon winning a round.
- **Monsters**– A random unit card will remain on the board following each round.
- **Scoia'tael** – Players are able to choose who goes first.

When building a deck, it's a good idea to remember that a deck must contain at least 22 unit cards and this can feature up to 10 special cards. Making your deck as small as possible will increase your chances of drawing the better cards Geralt has at his disposal.

Below you'll find a breakdown of the four card factions in the game and a brief overview of what you can expect from playing with them:

#### Northern Realms

The Northern Realms will be your starting deck and once you get the hang of it and flesh it out with a few more high-profile cards, it is also arguably the best deck in the game. Some key elements to take into account when running with a Northern Realms deck:

Foltest is the Leader card of the Northern Realms deck and he has a number of useful abilities depending on the variant that you play with. They include:

- **The Steel-Forged -** Trigger a Scorch effect on back (Siege) row units if unit strength is 10 or greater.
- **The Siege Master -** Trigger a Commander's Horn effect on your back (Siege) row units.
- **Lord Commander of the North –** Trigger a Clear Weather effect to remove all weather cards in play.
- **King of Temeria -** Allows you to pick an Impenetrable Fog card from your deck and play it instantly.

Some general thoughts, tips and interesting stuff about playing with a Northern Realms deck:

- Winning a round using a Northern Realms deck will allow you to draw another card, which is good for keeping a card advantage over your opponent.
- The deck has three Spy ability cards which will allow you to draw a large number of cards if you get them all together. Couple these with Decoys and you have a recipe for success.
- Using a selection of cards with the Tight Bond ability (Blue Stripes Commando, Catapult, Crinfrid Reavers Dragon Hunter) and placing all of them in play at once will greatly boost your overall attack score. Couple a few Catapults with Tight Bond with with a Commander's Horn and Foltest's Siege Master ability allows the faction to quickly rack up some big strength numbers, making it a powerful opponent if used correctly.
- The Northern Realm deck has access to a few Medic cards. Using these to resurrect other Medic ability cards or discarded spy cards will again give you a distinct advantage.

#### Monsters

The Monsters deck is a tricky one to get a handle on because of its abundance of melee enemies and its focus on the muster special ability. It's also not a very good to begin with and you'll need to work towards getting a few heroes and monster cards in your deck before trying it out. Some key elements to take into account when running with a Monsters faction deck:

Eredin is the Leader card of the Monsters deck and he has a number of useful abilities depending on the variant that you play with. They include:

- **Destroyer of Worlds -** Pick a card from your discard pile and put it back into your hand
- **Bringer of Death -** Discard two cards from your hand and in turn choose and draw 1 card from the deck.
- **King of the Wild Hunt –** Trigger a Commander's Horn effect on your front (infantry) row units.
- **Commander of the Red Riders -** Pick any weather card from the deck and play it instantly.

Some general thoughts, tips and interesting stuff about playing with a Monsters deck:

- Winning a round using a Monsters deck will allow one random unit card to stay in play for the next round. As such you'll want to try and win the first round to make use of that advantage.
- Monsters have no medic or spies and no real ability to draw cards so will rely on Muster to pull cards into play. This is very difficult to maintain over three rounds.
- Muster is the core of the Monsters faction. To make good use of the Muster ability, you'll want to have duplicates of the cards featuring that ability ( **Vampires, Arachas, Crones, Ghouls, Nekkers**). If you have the entire suite of like-named Muster cards, a single play can pull 3-5 Cards into play and place huge strength numbers (up to 21 with all five **Vampires**) onto the board in a single turn.
- As the majority of the Monster deck is melee, frontline units it's a good idea to hold onto a Clear Weather card in your deck to remove the inevitable Biting Frost cards opponents will no doubt roll out.

#### Nilfgaardian Empire

The Nilfgaardian Empire plays in a similar fashion to the Northern Realms deck and can utilise similar strategies revolving around the use of Decoys and Spy cards. Some key elements to take into account when running with a Nilfgaardian Empire deck:

Emyhr is the Leader card of the Nilfgaardian Empire deck and he has a number of useful abilities depending on the variant that you play with. They include:

- **The Relentless** \- Allows you to draw a card from the opponent's discard pile.
- **The White Flame** \- Allows you to cancel out your opponent's Leader ability when used.
- **The Emperor of Nilfgaard** \- Enables you to observe three random cardsin your opponent's hand.
- **His Imperial Majesty** \- Allows you to pick an Torrential Rain card from your deck and play it instantly.

Some general thoughts, tips and interesting stuff about playing with a Nilfgaardian Empire deck:

- The special ability of the Nilfgaardian Empire deck is that you technically do not have to win any round as you'll receive the win in the event of a draw.
- As with the Northern Realms faction, this deck has three Spy ability cards which will allow you to draw a large number of cards if you get them all together. Couple these with Decoys and you have a recipe for success.
- The Nilfgaardian Empire decks has access to a lot of Medic cards. Using these to resurrect other Medic ability cards or discarded spy cards will again give you a distinct advantage.

#### Scoia'tael

The Scoia'tael faction, like the Monsters deck is a little tricky to use. Whilst Northern Realms and Nilfgaardian Empire can get away with similar tactics, Scoia'tael provide something different to try out and the deck features a good mix of Muster, Agile and Medic cards. It should be noted that the deck itself is definitely not a starter deck and will need quite a few cards (and hopefully heroes as well) to choose from before you can construct a decent deck that will have you winning with regularity. Some key elements to take into account when running with a Scoia'tael deck:

Francesca is the Leader card of the Scoia'tael deck and she has a number of useful abilities depending on the variant that you play with. They include:

- **Queen of Dol Blathanna** – Trigger a Scorch effect on front (Infantry) row units if unit strength is 10 or greater.
- **The Beautiful** \- Trigger a Commander's Horn effect on your middle (Ranged) row units.
- **Daisy of the Valley** – You can draw one additional card at the start of the game.
- **Pureblood Elf** – Allows you to pick a Biting Frost card from your deck and play it instantly.

Some general thoughts, tips and interesting stuff about playing with a Scoia'tael deck:

- The Scoia'tael faction's special ability will allow you to choose which player will play first each round. This is a good opportunity to force your opponent to start so that you can get an early insight into what kind of strategy they are likely to use. Alternatively, you can start laying down big cards first in an attempt to set the tone of the round.
- As with the Monsters deck, the Scoia'tael deck has no Spy cards and no other ability to draw cards so you'll need to rely on Medic reviving and Muster to pull cards into play.
- The central focus of the Scoia'tael deck is the Agile ability. This will allow you to place most of the faction's unit cards on either the front or middle rows of the table. This is useful when stacking unit cards for a damage boost via a Commander's Horn and is also useful in mitigating or minimising the impact of weather cards on your units.
- To make good use of the Muster ability, you'll want to have duplicates of the cards featuring that ability ( **Elven Skirmishers, Dwarven Skirmishers, Havekar Smugglers**). If you have the entire suite of like-named Muster cards, a single play can pull three cards and a huge boost to your strength numbers (up to 15 with three **Havekar Smugglers**) onto the board in a single turn.

### General Hints and Tips

Here are some general tips to help introduce you to the world of Gwent. I'll be the first to admit that I myself am not all that great at the side-game but I found the following extremely useful and would have liked to have known/figured them out much sooner.

> **Note:** If you have a handy tip or hint, scroll down to the contact section and swing me an email.

- Gwent is not the easiest game to get the hang of, especially if you aren't a fan of card games. Spend some time on low/no wager matches so that you can learn the ins and outs of each deck and special abilities.
- You'll want to experiment with deck building and once you have enough cards, try playing with the different factions until you find one that you feel comfortable using.
- Be sure to visit every merchant/innkeeper when you visit a new area and purchase all of the Gwent cards in their inventory. Playing against merchants/innkeepers and winning for the first time will also net you a random card to add to your collection.
- Don't be afraid to pass and gift the opponent the first round. You only have limited cards, so make sure you don't waste them if you have no chance of winning!
- There's no shame in going into a card duel for the sole purpose of losing, just so that you can see what kind of deck, cards and strategy an opponent might be running. Afterwards, prepare your deck accordingly for a rematch.
- Try to get your opponent to waste cards! They can't win the latter rounds if they have no cards left! A good technique is to move some high value cards and decoys into your deck. Placing a high attack value card on the field to drive up their card numbers and then use decoy to return it to your hand before passing.
- Substitute standard unit cards with Hero/Neutral cards as soon as you get them. Hero/Neutral cards are more powerful and also less likely to be susceptible to special abilities.
- Unit cards with the Spy ability are an excellent addition to any deck. Although you'll place them on the opponent's side of the table, they allow you to draw two cards which is a nice bonus. If you follow that up with a well-timed weather card, this will essentially mitigate your opponent's advantage.
- If an opponent places a unit card with the Spy ability on your side of the table, use a Decoy card to grab it for yourself!
- Muster is a highly useful ability, but for it to be most effective, you'll need to load your deck with cards of the same variety whose name matches the unit cards with the Muster ability.
- Medics can be quite powerful - using a Medic card to revive another card with the Medic ability will have that trigger as well, effectively enabling players to revive multiple unit cards in a single turn.

After purchasing all three of these items, the achievement/trophy will unlock. In addition to this, you'll also unlock a new side-quest **‘Avid Collector'**.

Check out the walkthrough above for **[‘Open Sesame! – Part 1'](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Open%20Sesame!%20-%20Part%201)** for more information.

#### Curator of Nightmares

Description: Recreate all of Iris' nightmares in the Painted World.

This achievement/trophy can be attempted during the **‘Scenes From a Marriage'** quest and can be missed if you progress too far into, or complete the quest before earning it. During the second half of the quest, you'll be transported to the painted world.

Here you'll need to locate a series of incomplete memories and re-create these memories using items in the environment. There are eight of these in total, but only seven of them are required to complete the quest. You'll earn the achievement/trophy by solving all eight (including the optional/missable) memories. They can be found in the following locations:

- In the gazebo in the back courtyard by the starting point (this is the optional/missable memory)
- By the fountain in the back courtyard.
- In the dining room.
- In the bed chamber.
- In the painting studio.
- In Olgierd's study.
- In the wine cellar/basement.
- In the dining room (Winter).

Upon solving all eight of the memories, the achievement/trophy will unlock.

Check out the walkthrough above for **[‘Scenes From a Marriage'](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Scenes%20From%20a%20Marriage)** for more information.

#### When its Many Against One…

**Description:** Provoke all Iris von Everec's Nightmares into fighting you at the same time and defeat them.

This achievement/trophy can be attempted during the **‘Scenes From a Marriage'** quest and can be missed if you complete the quest before earning it. At the very end of the quest you'll enter into a boss fight against a series of Ethereal Nightmare creatures. There are six of these in total and they will only attack one at a time in order, unless provoked. This achievement/trophy requires us to provoke all six of the Ethereals by hitting them with **AARD/IGNI** or a sword strike and then fight them all at once at once.

This is incredibly difficult, especially on Death March or Blood and Broken Bones difficulty, hence you should save your game as soon as you are finished completing the memories. Don't be afraid to lower the difficulty for this fight just to earn the achievement/trophy before reloading your save and trying again on your difficulty of choice if you have no luck.

Check out the walkthrough above for **[‘Scenes From a Marriage'](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/xboxone/142226-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-hearts-of-stone/faqs/74361#Scenes%20From%20a%20Marriage)** for more detailed information on the boss encounter and how to defeat it.

#### I Wore Ofieri Before it Was Cool

**Description:** Collect all available Ofieri armor and horse gear, and at least one Ofieri sword.

To earn this achievement/trophy you'll be required to completely deck out Geralt and Roach with Ofieri gear in every equippable item slot. Fortunately, this is not all that difficult, as you'll be able to earn all of what you need from the Merchant at the **‘Upper Mill'** fast travel marker or the pair of side-quests that he gives you ( **‘From** Ofier's **Distant Shores'** and **‘Races: As Swift as the Western Wind'**). You'll need to acquire the following items:

- Ofieri **Stock Saddle** – complete the **‘Races: Swift as the Western Wind'** side-quest.
- **Ofieri Boots** – Complete the **‘From** Ofier's Distand **Shores'** side-quest for the crafting diagram.
- Ofieri **Gauntlets** – Complete the **‘From** Ofier's Distand **Shores'** side-quest for the crafting diagram.
- Ofieri **Scale Armor** – Complete the **‘From** Ofier's Distand **Shores'** side-quest for the crafting diagram.
- Ofieri **Sharovary** – Complete the **‘From** Ofier's Distand **Shores'** side-quest for the crafting diagram.
- Ofieri **Blinders** – Can be purchased from the Ofieri Merchant at the **‘Upper Mill'**.
- Ofieri **Saddlebags** – Can be purchased from the Ofieri Merchant at the **‘Upper Mill'**.
- Ofieri **Sword** – Can either be found as random loot in chests or purchased from the Ofieri Merchant at the **‘Upper Mill'**.

After recieving them from the Ofieri merchant, the four armor diagrams will have to be crafted by a master blacksmith – the one in Crow's Perch should be sufficient as long as you've completed the **‘Master Armorers'** side-quest previously.

Once you have found and accumulated all of the required gear, equip all of it at the same time to unlock the **‘I Wore** Ofieri **Before it Was Cool' achievement/trophy**.

#### Wild Rose Dethorned

**Description:** Defeat all the fallen knights and loot their campsites.

This achievement/trophy requires that you track down and clear out the enemies from all eight of the new Bandit Camps (and looting their treasure) in the new region unlocked by the Hearts of Stone expansion. Grabbing the **‘I Wore** Ofieri **Before it Was Cool'** side-quest will require you to take out four of these Bandit Camps, so it's a good idea to pick that side-quest up before attempting this one.

The Bandit Camps can be found in the following locations:

- In the hills directly north of the **‘Garin Estate'** fast travel marker.
- Across the river to the north of the **‘Upper Mill'** fast travel marker and in the hills to the northwest of the abandoned church.
- In an elven ruin located to the southwest of the **‘Zueter Castle'** and across the river to the east of the **‘Erde'** fast travel markers.
- Just to the east of the **‘Est** Tayiar **'** and south of the **‘Vikk Watchtower'** fast travel markers.
- just west of the **‘Erde'** and directly south of the **‘Von Everec Estate'** fast travel markers.
- In a ruined castle along the road just to the south of the **‘Upper Mill'** fast travel marker.
- located north of the town of Bowdon which is located just to the north of the **‘Isolated Hut'** and northwest of the **‘Garin Estate'** fast travel markers.

After completing all eight of the Bandit Camps, the achievement/trophy will unlock.

#### Rad Steez Bro!

**Description:** Slide downhill uninterrupted for at least 10 seconds.

This achievement/trophy is fairly easy to figure out what to do, but is a little trickier when it comes to finding a location at which we can actually perform it as there are few slopes in the game capable of allowing 10 seconds of continuous sliding.

Fortunately, there is an easy place to do it. If you have undertaken the **‘The Path of Warriors'** side-quest (can be obtained by speaking to NPCs by the travel marker for **‘Urialla Harbor'** on An Skellig, the large north-eastern island of the Skellige Isles) you should have a fast travel marker for **‘Yngvar's Fang'**, which is located at the very top of a mountain on An Skellig.

From the **‘Yngvar's Fang'** fast travel marker, follow the small set of stairs to the right until they disappear and slide down the slope here to reach the entrance to the cave at the bottom. This slope will be sufficient to unlock the **‘Rad Steez Bro!' achievement/trophy**.

#### Moo-rderer

**Description:** Kill 20 cows.

To earn this achievement/trophy we'll simply need to find and kill 20 cows. There are plenty of cows in the game world, but if you are in a rush to get the achievement trophy, there are a few places that can assist in doing so fairly quickly. These include:

To earn this achievement/trophy we'll simply need to find and kill 20 cows. There are plenty of cows in the game world, but if you are in a rush to get the achievement trophy, there are a few places that can assist in doing so fairly quickly. These include:

- The fields just south of the **'Yantra'** fast travel marker contain more than a dozen cows.
- The fields by the **‘Blandare'** fast travel marker in Ard Skellige (the main island) features 8-10 Cows.

Pick a field, kill all of the cows in it and then meditate for 24 hours and all of the cows will respawn. Kill them again! Continue until the achievement/trophy pops for you.

#### Return to Sender

**Description:** Kill 3 opponents with their own arrows.

In order to unlock this achievement/trophy, you'll need to first unlock and max out the **‘Arrow Deflection'** Skill in the **Defence** section of the Combat Skill Tree. This will enable us to perform a block just as an arrow is about to hit Geralt. Successfully blocking the arrow will send it back, hitting the enemy that fired it. As the deflected arrows are unlikely to kill enemies right away, you may need to counter multiple arrows from each attacker or hit them with a melee attack or two first before trying the deflection out.

It probably goes without saying, but arrows fly pretty quickly. As such, it is best to attempt deflections from a larger distance to give yourself some additional reaction time.

We'll need to accumulate a total of 3 kills using this method in order to unlock this achievement/trophy.

* * *

#### Can Quit Anytime I Want

**Description:** Be under the influence of seven potions or decoctions at the same time.

Before attempting this achievement/trophy we'll need to do a bit of preparation. You'll need to first unlock and max out the **‘Acquired Tolerance'** Skill in the **Mutations** section of the Alchemy Skill Tree to greatly boost your overall toxicity level.

Afterwards, look through your available potions and consume the seven in your inventory with the least amount of toxicity.

* * *

#### Killed It

**Description:** Win a round of Gwent with a total strength of at least 187.

The description is relatively self-explanatory. You'll need to play Gwent and win with the strength score for a round above 187. This can only really be achieved with a top tier deck and a lot of luck - as the cards you draw will ultimately determine how much strength you can lay out. I was able to obtain this value using the Monsters, Nilfgaardian Empire and Northern Realms decks. I did however, find the Northern Realms deck by far the easiest with which to do it. This is because of the large number of Tight Bond cards in the deck which when played together alongside a Commander's Horn can put up some staggeringly large figures (especially with the siege engines of the Northern Realms deck).

Ultimately, how you obtain this strength value is up to you and how you play Gwent and will come with time if you actively try to do it with the cards you've been dealt.
