## About the game

Monster Hunter Stories is a game designed for Nintendo 3DS, published by Capcom and licensed by Nintendo. It was rated E10+ by the Entertainment Software Rating Board and received content descriptors for crude humor and fantasy violence. The game is an RPG with monster-collecting elements with 3D open-world exploration. Unlike other games in the Monster Hunter franchise, Stories uses a turn-based strategy combat system with a 2Vn setup. The game also features elements of resource management. The game was released exclusively for the Nintendo 3DS console on October 8, 2017.

## Controls

Menu controls

- D+pad, Circle Pad: Select
- ZL,ZR: Page
- A: Accept
- B: Back

Overworld controls

- Circle Pad: move avatar
- D+pad, L, R, C-nub: camera
- Y: mount Monstie (field only)
- B: crouch (field only), field skill (while mounted)
- A: interact
- ZL: Navirou
- ZR: Monsties
- X: camp menu

Special items and data can be imported from the demo version.

- New game: Start a new game
- Load game: (This option is faded out at the start. Saving a game will add statistics to the upper screen)

## One Year Later

**Hakum Village**

There is one new facility in the village that is available to you, the **Prayer Pot**. Using this can grant you stat bonuses for a short period of time. Ignore it for now and talk to dan. He will direct you to the ritual site.

**Ritual site**

Speak to the elder to begin the ritual.

Scene

While tapping the A button will hatch the egg, it is recommended that you use a stylus for this activity, as certain statistical boosts can only be obtained by tapping certain parts of the egg. After the egg hatches, you will receive a new baby **Velocidrome**. After another conversation, head outside.

**Hakum Village**

After proceeding down the path, Navirou will ask to see your house.

**Your House**

Inside your house you can use the nearby chest to change your hair and eye color, as well as dye your armor and view previously seen cutscenes. There is also a bed witch acts as a save point, and Navirou will stand in between the two while you’re in here and give you info on your current story mission. After you’re done, go outside.

**Hakum Village**

You'll also be able to talk to the **Courier** and **Connecticat**. These characters handle Streetpass and Wi-Fi connectivity respectively. They’re useless in the demo version, so talk to Dan to continue, then head out to Pondry Hills

**Pondry Hills**

Follow the path until you encounter Dan again. He’ll ask you to engage the nearby **Aptonath** in battle. These next few moments serve as a battle tutorial. Defeat the Aptonath to move on.

Over the hill you’ll encounter a Scripted **Velociprey**. While the Aptonath used power attacks, Velociprey focus on Speed attacks. Use technical attacks to defeat it. After defeating it, the screen will transition to skip the majority of the walk to the caves. After a bit more advice from Dan, head down the path towards the cave. On the way you’ll encounter a treasure chest. Unlike other collectibles, these large treasure chests will not reset upon transitions. You may also encounter wild **Kelbies** on the way. Kelbies are small monsters, and will flee when approached, occasionally dropping items. Kelbies cannot be engaged in battle.

Heading down the path, you’re likely to encounter two Velociprey guarding the entrance to the cave. If they attack you while your back is turned, they’ll get a free attack on you. However, you can achieve the same by engaging them from behind. Either take them down or sneak past them into the caves.

**Pondry Caves**

Once inside, head to the right fork and continue down the corridor to find another treasure chest. You’ll also encounter a couple **Konchu (yellow)**. Continue to the next room where you’ll find another chest and a **Lost Poogie**. There are one hundred of these piglets scattered across the world. They’ll do a little dance before leaving behind an item. Interact with the waterfall to continue.

Scene

There is a scripted battle with a black **Nargacuga**. This might be a difficult battle, so remember to use herbs to heal you and your monstie. After the battle, you’ll be taken back to the village.

**Hakum Village**

You’ll now be able to use the **weapon smith** and **item trader**. You’ll have 1000 zenny to spend, and you’ll be able to sell items to gain more. At the weapon smith, you can buy new weapons and armor, as well as upgrade your existing gear using materials collected out in the field. The item trader will sell you useful items to use. NPCs with an orange “...” bubble are useful to speak to, but aren’t necessary to continue the main quest. After you’re done shopping, speak to Dan to continue the story. You’ll also be able to accept sub quests by talking to characters with blue “!” Above their heads as well as using the bulletin board near the path out to Pondry Hills. The quests given to you by the characters around town serve as short tutorials for the field collectibles. I suggest talking to the Worrywart Rider and accept the **Enigma of the Egg** sub quest, as it will be easily completed easily with the item you’ll receive at the end of the story quest.

**Pondry Hills**

You’ll now be able to ride you’re monstie while in the field. Certain Monsties have field skills that can be used to access new areas or search the field for monsters and collectibles. Follow the marker to a **Jump Pad**. When standing on these while riding a monstie with the jump skill, you can press A to leap across large gaps. After jumping across, you’ll come to a **Monster Den**. Monster dens are small mini-dungeons with an egg nest at the end. Depending on the situation you may have to sneak past a sleeping monster or battle one outright to get to the egg nest. Interact with the nest to search for an egg. You can discard the current egg you’re holding for a chance to find a better egg, but there are a limited amount of eggs in the nest, and once you discard an egg, you can’t pick it up again. As this area is for the main quest, you’re likely to only find **Humble Herbiv Eggs**. You won’t be told what egg you’re carrying until you leave the den, so you might need a guide to find the egg you’re looking for. Once you’re out, return to Hakum Village to continue.

**Hakum Village**

I recommend speaking to the worrywart rider before hatching your egg to complete his sub quest. Quests from NPCs that are ready to be turned in will be marked with a white “!” Bubble.

Talk to the Stable Paw to hatch your egg. Like all Calicos, he’ll use a wealth of cat-related puns when speaking to him. Your new **Aptonath** will be added to your party automatically. Dan will tell you to see the chief, then you’ll talk to Dan again to learn about combining. After crafting five potions, he’ll tell you to head to Whistill Forest. Monster dens will now appear in the field. You can search these egg nests to find **Humble Fng Beast eggs**, **Humble Brd Wyv eggs**, and **Burning Brd Wyv eggs**. these will award you with new Monsties **Arzuros**, another Velocidrome, and **Yian Kut-Ku** respectively. You might even find the rare **Blue Yian Kut-Ku** variant. while variants will have different color eggs, they’ll have the same title as their standard types. You may want to hold off on turning in your quests until you fill out your roster. You can also use monster bait to level up your monsties individually. Once you’re done exploring Pondry Hills, head to whistill forest to continue the story.

At the old Mine, you’ll encounter a Calico known as The **Catavaner.** He’ll task you with tracking down the wheel for his cart. ## The Road To Albarax

**The Hunter’s Guild**

The Guildmaster has tasked you with heading for Albarax in the Trese Desert. The entrance to the desert from the plains is marked with trees covered in autumn leaves.

**Trese Desert**

Check in with Reverto in Oasum first. He’ll tell you to defeat two monsters causing quicksand to block the entrance to Albarax. To the West you’ll find a **Daimyo Hermitaur**. This giant Hermit crab occasionally uses **Guard**, which in the case of this monster, sacrifices his turn choice to counter any attack made against it for three turns. If you know it will counter, don’t use attacks. Your best bet is to use the times it guards to heal and buff your party. To the east is a cave, where you’ll encounter a **Barroth**. This monster will use speed attacks until it uses **Mudroll**, at which point it will alter its attack resistance and switch to power attacks. Remember that its attack skill is rated as speed, so keep an eye out. Once you defeat both, head back to Oasum.

**Oasum**

Scene

Once you’re done here, head to the quicksand outside of Albarax.

**Trese Desert**

Out here you’ll encounter a **Blighted Cephadrome**. This Blighted sand shark is far more powerful than its counterparts scattered around the desert. After defeating it, you’ll have to fight **Barroth EX**. This heavily armored monster uses special skills constantly, so it might be difficult to get a head-to-head with it. You’ll be automatically healed after both battles, so head up the stairs into Albarax when you’re done.

**Albarax**

Scene

This **Blighted Diablos** is the hardest, longest battle yet. It features two destructible parts, and when it grows angry, it’ll attack twice in one turn. Make sure you’re stocked up on potions to heal your team. Once you defeat it, you’ll purify the kinship ore here and gain acces to level 4 rarity monsters.

Scene

Once you clear the spring, the elder will task you with calling the Grand Felyne to help with the restoration of Albarax. You’ll gain use of the **Monster Expedition**, where you send out monsters that are not in your party to collect materials and experience. This helps in training new Monsties to put on your team. The process takes up to 30 real-time minutes, so pass the time by completing Subquests. Once the time has expired, speak to the elder to finish the town. Lilia will now ask you to return to Gildegaran with her.

## Naubaka Island

## Two Kinship ores

Hall of Scriveners

You‘ll have two stones to find, one in Naubaka island, and the other in the Babda Rainforest. You’ll see the marker at the docks first, however you can do them in any order you want.

## Tips about the game

Here are just a few tips to remember while playing the game

**what weapon do I use?**

It’s ultimately up to you. There doesn’t appear to be a large benefit to using any weapon above anything else. You will have to upgrade your weapon and replace it with higher ranked weapons, but other than that, the weapons appear primarily for role-playing aspects.

the same thing can be said about armor. Until a certain point the only reason to purchase new armor is a higher base defense. They will have specific elemental strengths and weaknesses, but as most of the game relies on the Head-To-Head mechanic, type advantage is not nearly as important in this game compared to something like Pokémon.

**what’s the best way you’ll o beat a Boss?**

the only bosses you’ll encounter in the game are the EX and Blighted variants of normal monsters. While each monster does have a preferred attack out of the triangle, however their behavior becomes more erratic and difficult to predict the larger monsters are. Ultimately, trying to memorize the patterns will take longer than just fighting a monster.

**What moves do I use?**

if you or your Monstie is to face off with a specific enemy, a red beam is formed between the two combatants. It won’t tell you what attack the opponent is using until you have confirmed your decision. Only basic attacks and skills aligned to one of the three basic attack types will form a beam. Special attacks, denoted by a yellow command box, will not result in a Head-To-Head.

**is there really no best strategy to the game?**

the short answer is no. Regardless of how you play the game, you’re likely to spend around 40-50 hours finishing the story. The only way to reliably defeat your opponent faster is to predict their attack in a Head-To-Head, and that becomes increasingly difficult as the patterns become more erratic and use less of these basic attacks. The game feels almost too perfectly balanced. Trying to save time in one area will ultimately lead to you spending more time in another aspect of the game.

As for bosses, watch for the red line between characters, and you or your Monstie will have a one in three chance of dealing heavy damage, dealing light damage, or bouncing off of each other, and dealing damage. During a head-to-head, damage is dealt simultaneously, so you take damage even if you defeat the opponent. You do have two methods of completely negating an enemy attack. 1) Perform a double attack. These are really rare as you can only perform one when both you and your monstie perform the same advantageous normal attack against the opponent. It cannot be combined with a special attack skill. You also cannot command your Monsties to use normal attacks, so you simply have to get lucky. 2) Use your kinship skill. These can only be performed by a joined rider and Monstie, and while joined you can only perform normal attacks or your Kinship skill. Since you need a full charge to combine and perform kinship skills, you’ll have to grind a lot of low damage to get there. Not that any boss has an attack you absolutely want to avoid getting hit by. Either the damage you take is moderate, or Every attack puts you on death’s door, which means you simply need to go out and grind.

## Weapons and armor guide

Here will be a list of all weapons and armor in the game. Weapons And armor can be purchased from weapon smiths in towns, or forged using monster parts. You can also upgrade a weapon’s base attack and armor’s base defense using monster parts and Zenny. Weapons are listed by type and rank. There are a list of basic statistics for weapons and armor you should know.

**weapons**

- **Name:** The name of the weapon
- **Rank:** The Rarity rank of the weapon
- **Attack:** The weapon’s base Attack
- **Element:** The elemental strength
- **Crit:** The Critical hit ratio, measured in percentage
- **Bonus:** The bonus effect
- **Skll:** An attack skill unique to that weapon
- **Obtain: T** he Zenny/material cost of the weapon
- **Level:** The strength based on how much Zenny/material has been invested

**Armor**

- **Name:** the name of the armor
- **Rank:** The Rarity rank
- **Defense:** The armor’s base defense
- **Fire Res:** The amount of resistance to fire attack skills
- **Water Res:** The amount of resistance to water attack skills
- **Elec Res:** The amount of resistance to Electrical attack skills
- **Ice Res:** The amount of resistance to Ice attack skills
- **Dragon Res:** The amount of resistance to dragon attack skills
- **Skills:** Unique boosts to character stats
- **Obtain:** The Zenny/material cost of the armor
- **Level:** The strength based on how much Zenny/material has been invested
